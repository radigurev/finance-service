## 1. Context & Scope

This spec defines **payment allocation** — the sub-ledger matching that records which invoices a payment settles, and by how much — and the **settlement state** that matching derives. It owns two entities: `PaymentAllocation` (the match row) and `InvoiceOpenItem` (a local, event-fed read projection of the invoices a payment may be matched against). SDD-PAY-001 owns the `Payment` aggregate, its lifecycle, its numbering, and its posting handshake; this spec adds only the matching layer on top of it.

Allocation answers the AP/AR question "what does this counterparty still owe (or what are we still owed)" without touching the general ledger. A confirmed payment has already moved cash and the AR/AP control account through its own journal entry (SDD-PAY-001 → SDD-FIN-006); a confirmed invoice has already moved the same control account in the opposite direction (SDD-INV-001). Allocation is the **assertion of which control-account movements offset which** — a sub-ledger opinion, not a ledger fact.

Three principles govern allocation:
1. **Allocation is matching, NOT posting.** An allocate or deallocate MUST NOT create, mutate, or reverse a journal entry, MUST NOT change any GL or trial-balance figure (SDD-FIN-003), and MUST NOT invoke `IWorkflowEngine<Payment>`. Because nothing is posted, allocation rows are deliberately **mutable and removable** — a mis-match is corrected by deleting the row and creating the right one, NOT by a sign-flipped reversal (contrast SDD-FIN-002 §2.6 / SDD-PAY-001, where a posted document is immutable).
2. **The invoice belongs to another service.** The invoice lives in `finance_invoices` (SDD-INV-001). `PaymentAllocation.InvoiceId` is therefore a cross-service GUID reference with **no foreign key**, and outstanding amounts MUST NEVER be obtained by a cross-database join (Plan §8) nor by a synchronous read on the write path. They come from a **local projection** (`InvoiceOpenItem`) fed by the invoice's own domain events.
3. **Over-allocation is forbidden, not tolerated.** The sum of a payment's allocations MUST NOT exceed the payment amount, and the sum of an invoice's allocations MUST NOT exceed its gross total. Both bounds are compared as exact `DECIMAL(18,2)` values. Because over-allocation cannot happen, v1 needs no tolerance band, no residual write-off, and no unapplied-cash suspense rule.

**Eventual consistency is explicit, not hidden.** The `InvoiceOpenItem` projection lags the Invoices service by the outbox delivery latency (outbox `QueryDelay` 1s + broker + consume). An invoice confirmed moments ago MAY not yet be allocatable, and that MUST surface as `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` — a legitimate transient outcome the caller retries, NOT a defect and NOT a reason to reintroduce a synchronous read-through to the Invoices service (which is exactly the availability coupling the projection exists to remove). A nightly reconciliation job that re-syncs drifted or lost open items MAY be added later; it is DEFERRED (§7).

**The realized-FX seam is dormant by design.** Allocation REQUIRES payment currency == invoice currency, so no transactional conversion happens while matching. When the payment's `ExchangeRate` differs from the invoice's `BookingExchangeRate`, however, the allocated portion carries different **base-currency values on the two documents' own frozen rates**. That is a DOCUMENT-level (reporting) difference, not a general-ledger one: the shipped posting path is currency-naive, so there are no rate-converted GL base balances for it to reconcile (§2.9). v1 computes the difference from the two aggregates' stored rates, stores it on the allocation row as `RealizedFxDifference`, and hands it to an `IRealizedFxHandler` seam whose only registered implementation is a no-op. Posting the difference to a GL gain/loss account is DEFERRED to the not-yet-authored SDD-FIN-005. Until SDD-FIN-005 lands the value is **informational only**. This mirrors SDD-FIN-002 §2.7, which shipped `IPostingPeriodGuard` + an always-open default so posting worked end-to-end while SDD-FIN-004 was unbuilt.

**ISA-95 classification.** A `PaymentAllocation` is an ISA-95 **Level 4 (Business Planning & Logistics)** financial **transaction-line / settlement record** that links two Level-4 financial Documents — a Payment (SDD-PAY-001) and an Invoice (SDD-INV-001) — and carries the matched amount (ISA-95 / IEC 62264 Part 1, §5). The **allocate** and **deallocate** operations are Level-4 business transactions that change recorded settlement state; each MUST emit an **immutable domain event** (`PaymentAllocatedEvent`, `PaymentDeallocatedEvent`) and an immutable audit row (SDD-AUDIT-001). `SettlementStatus` is a **derived** Level-4 document state, computed from two decimals — not a stored workflow state. An `InvoiceOpenItem` is NOT a new business-transaction record: it is a Level-4 **read projection** of another service's Document, created only from that Document's own immutable events, so it needs no event and no audit row of its own. The counterparty is Warehouse-owned Level-4 master data referenced by GUID, NOT a foreign key (Plan §8). No Level-3 (MES) activity is modelled.

**Scope — covered (v1):**
- The `PaymentAllocation` entity (`INT IDENTITY` PK, schema `payments`) and its composition under the `Payment` aggregate.
- The `InvoiceOpenItem` local read projection (PK = the mirrored `InvoiceId`), its FOUR idempotent MassTransit consumers of the invoice events — the three shipped ones plus `InvoiceReversedEvent`, which the SDD-INV-001 amendment publishes in this batch so a voided invoice cannot stay `Posted` (and therefore allocatable) in the projection — the **settleable-document-type admission rule** that keeps a `CreditNote` (a type no `SettlementPairing` entry can settle) out of the projection entirely (§2.3), and the explicitly-stated eventual-consistency contract.
- `POST /api/v1/payments/{id}/allocations` (explicit invoice + amount list), `DELETE /api/v1/payments/{id}/allocations/{allocationId}`, `GET /api/v1/payments/{id}/allocations`.
- The full cross-aggregate invariant chain as an `IChainValidator<PaymentAllocationValidationContext>` chain (SDD-INFRA-007), with one error code per rule (§2.5) — including the control-account pairing rule that makes a match an ACCOUNTING match, not merely a same-cash-direction one.
- The derived `SettlementStatus` (`Unsettled` / `PartiallySettled` / `Settled`) by exact decimal comparison, and the maintenance of `Payment.AllocatedAmount` + `InvoiceOpenItem.SettledAmount`.
- `PaymentAllocatedEvent` / `PaymentDeallocatedEvent` through the EF transactional outbox and the one-way settlement handshake to the Invoices service.
- The realized-FX computation + storage and the dormant `IRealizedFxHandler` seam.
- Audit-first writes, RBAC, correlation, no caching, and the DB design + migration for both tables.

**Scope — excluded (deferred):**
- **The `Payment` aggregate, its lifecycle, numbering, and posting** — SDD-PAY-001. This spec reads `Status`, `Direction`, `CounterpartyId`, `CurrencyCode`, `ExchangeRate`, `Amount` and maintains `AllocatedAmount`; it defines none of them.
- **The invoice-side settlement update** (the invoice's own `SettledAmount`/`SettlementStatus`, and the additive `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` fields) — the SDD-INV-001 amendment. This spec's obligation ends at its outbox row.
- **AP/AR aging, counterparty balances, and the open-item list endpoint** — SDD-PAY-003 (read-only over this projection; no new tables).
- **Posting the realized FX difference** to a gain/loss account — SDD-FIN-005. v1 stores the number and posts nothing.
- **Cross-currency allocation** (payment `EUR` against invoice `BGN`) — SDD-FIN-005; v1 rejects it with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_CURRENCY_MISMATCH`.
- **Credit-note settlement** (allocating cash against an `InvoiceDocumentType.CreditNote`) — DEFERRED with the refund / offset feature (SDD-PAY-001 §1 defers the refund document type and the `Compensation` method to a future `CHG-FEAT-*`), because a credit note's control account is not determinable from shipped code (§2.5 rule 10). Since no `SettlementPairing` entry can settle a `CreditNote`, v1 does not merely reject the allocation — the §2.3 projection consumer never creates an open item for one, so the attempt is rejected EARLIER, at chain rule 2, with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` (404). `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_CONTROL_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH` remains specified and tested but is UNREACHABLE through the v1 paths (§2.5).
- **FIFO / oldest-due-first auto-matching** and bulk "apply the whole payment" behavior — a future `CHG-FEAT-*`; v1 requires an explicit invoice list.
- **Settlement tolerance, residual write-off, and unapplied-cash suspense accounting** — a future `CHG-ENH-*`; over-allocation is forbidden so no residual can arise.
- **In-place amendment of an allocation's amount** — v1 requires deallocate + re-allocate; a `PUT .../allocations/{id}` is a future additive endpoint.
- **Counterparty statements, dunning, and credit limits** — Phase 7 (SDD-RPT-*).

## 2. Behavior

> **Service/controller contract (SDD-INFRA-009).** `PaymentAllocationService` MUST inherit `SearchableServiceBase<PaymentAllocation, PaymentAllocationDto, PaymentsDbContext>` (and thus `BaseEntityService<PaymentsDbContext>`) for the SDD-INFRA-005 list mechanics. Because the base `BuildBaseQuery()` takes no route argument, the payment-scoped list MUST be a dedicated method that runs the filter pipeline over an explicitly scoped `IQueryable` — the payment id MUST NOT be smuggled through mutable service state. Every public method MUST return `Result` / `Result<T>` — never `null`, never a thrown exception for a business outcome. `PaymentAllocationsController` inherits `BaseApiController` and translates every result via `ToActionResult(...)`. The service owns `SaveChanges` / `RowVersion` / the audit row / the outbox row inside ONE transaction; `IWorkflowEngine<Payment>` MUST NOT be invoked (§2.4). `CancellationToken` MUST be threaded controller → service → chain → DB / publish.

### 2.1 `PaymentAllocation` entity (MUST)
- The `PaymentAllocation` entity MUST carry:
  - `Id` (`INT IDENTITY`, PK `PK_PaymentAllocations`) — an internal child reference. An allocation row is never individually event-exposed (the events carry the `(PaymentId, InvoiceId)` pair, §2.10), so it MUST be `INT IDENTITY` and MUST NOT be a sequential GUID (CLAUDE.md §0.1).
  - `PaymentId` (`UNIQUEIDENTIFIER`, FK → `payments.Payments.Id`, `FK_PaymentAllocations_Payments`, `ON DELETE CASCADE`) — the owning payment (SDD-PAY-001).
  - `InvoiceId` (`UNIQUEIDENTIFIER`) — a CROSS-SERVICE reference to an invoice owned by `finance_invoices`. It MUST NOT be a foreign key and MUST NEVER be resolved by a cross-database join (Plan §8); existence is asserted against the local projection (§2.2).
  - `AllocatedAmount` (`DECIMAL(18,2)`) — the amount applied, in the payment's `CurrencyCode` (which equals the invoice's currency, §2.5). MUST be `> 0`; `0.00` and negative amounts MUST be rejected (§3.1).
  - `BaseAllocatedAmount` (`DECIMAL(18,2)`) — `AllocatedAmount × Payment.ExchangeRate`, rounded through `ICountryStrategy` (§2.9). Never `double`/`float`. It is a REPORTING figure only: allocation posts nothing (§1 principle 1) and the ledger stores no rate-converted base amounts (§2.9), so it MUST NOT be described as, or reconciled against, a journal-entry base amount.
  - `RealizedFxDifference` (`DECIMAL(18,2)`, default `0.00`) — the signed base-currency difference described in §2.9. Informational in v1.
  - `AllocatedAt` (`DATETIMEOFFSET`, `SYSDATETIMEOFFSET()` default) and `AllocatedBy` (`UNIQUEIDENTIFIER`, from `ICurrentUserAccessor`).
  - `CorrelationId` (`string`, max 100) — the ambient correlation id from `ICorrelationIdAccessor` (SDD-INFRA-001).
  - `RowVersion` (`byte[]`, `rowversion`) — optimistic-concurrency token.
- The `Payment` aggregate MUST own `ICollection<PaymentAllocation> Allocations` as a composition (cascade-deleted with the payment). The navigation MUST NOT be `AutoInclude()`d — the SDD-PAY-001 payment list MUST NOT fan out into allocation rows on every read.
- Filterable/sortable surface MUST be opt-in via `[Filterable]`/`[Sortable]`: `InvoiceId`, `AllocatedAmount`, `AllocatedAt` (SDD-INFRA-005).
- There MUST be no allocation status column: an allocation row either exists (matched) or does not (released). A `SettlementStatus` belongs to the invoice open item, NOT to the allocation row (§2.8).

### 2.2 `InvoiceOpenItem` local projection entity (MUST)
- `InvoiceOpenItem` MUST be a LOCAL read projection inside `finance_payments` so that allocation (§2.5) and aging (SDD-PAY-003) never cross-join another service's database and never depend on the Invoices service being reachable.
- `InvoiceId` (`UNIQUEIDENTIFIER`) MUST be the primary key (`PK_InvoiceOpenItems`) — the mirror of the external key. It MUST NOT have a surrogate identity column and MUST NOT be generated (`NEWSEQUENTIALID()` MUST NOT be configured): the value ALWAYS arrives on the source event.
- The projection MUST carry: `DocumentNumber` (max 40), `DocumentType` (string, max 30), `Direction` (string, max 2 — `AR`/`AP`), `CounterpartyId` (`UNIQUEIDENTIFIER`), `CurrencyCode` (3), `BaseCurrencyCode` (3), `GrossTotal` (`DECIMAL(18,2)`), `BookingExchangeRate` (`DECIMAL(18,6)`), `IssueDate` (`DATETIMEOFFSET`), `DueDate` (`DATETIMEOFFSET`), `InvoiceStatus` (string, max 20), `SettledAmount` (`DECIMAL(18,2)`, default `0.00`), `LastAppliedAt` (`DATETIMEOFFSET`), `RowVersion` (`rowversion`).
- The mirrored `InvoiceStatus` value set MUST be exactly `Confirmed`, `Posted`, `Cancelled`, `Reversed` — the four `InvoiceStatus` names (`src/Finance.Common/Enums/InvoiceStatus.cs`) reachable through the §2.3 events. `Draft` MUST NEVER appear: a draft invoice publishes no confirmation, and the only draft-originated row is the cancellation tombstone, which enters as `Cancelled` (§2.3). `Cancelled` and `Reversed` are TERMINAL in the projection exactly as they are on the invoice aggregate, and they are the two statuses §2.5 rule 3 rejects.
- `Outstanding` = `GrossTotal − SettledAmount` MUST be computed on read and MUST NOT be stored (it is derivable, and a stored copy would be a second source of truth).
- `SettledAmount` is **locally owned**: it MUST equal the sum of this database's `PaymentAllocation.AllocatedAmount` rows for that `InvoiceId`, and it MUST be maintained in the SAME transaction as the allocation write (§2.4/§2.6). The §2.3 consumers MUST NEVER write it. The Invoices service maintains its OWN copy from the events this spec publishes (SDD-INV-001 amendment) — the two are independent by design and MUST NOT be cross-checked synchronously.
- Every other column is **externally owned**: only the §2.3 consumers may write them, and no endpoint MUST expose a write path to them (there is no `POST /open-items`).
- `RowVersion` on the projection is REQUIRED: it is the serialization point for two different payments allocating against the SAME invoice concurrently (§2.14).
- Filterable/sortable surface MUST be opt-in via `[Filterable]`/`[Sortable]`: `DocumentNumber`, `Direction`, `CounterpartyId`, `CurrencyCode`, `InvoiceStatus`, `IssueDate`, `DueDate` — the surface SDD-PAY-003 queries.
- **Two additive source fields are REQUIRED.** The shipped `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` (SDD-INV-001 §2.11) carries neither `DueDate` nor a booking exchange rate. Both MUST be added to it as additive, non-breaking fields by the SDD-INV-001 amendment in the same batch (SDD-INV-001 §5: "adding an event field is additive"). `InvoiceConfirmedEvent.BookingExchangeRate` MUST be sourced from a REAL, frozen invoice-side rate — the amendment adds `ExchangeRate DECIMAL(18,6) NOT NULL DEFAULT 1.000000` to `invoices.Invoices` in its NEW additive `AddInvoiceSettlement` migration, alongside `SettledAmount`, the settlement status and `LastSettlementAppliedAt` (SDD-INV-001 §2.14), and freezes it at invoice creation from the transaction-date rate in the same WAY the pre-existing `BaseCurrencyCode` already is (SDD-INV-001 §2.3) — NOT in the same migration as `BaseCurrencyCode`, which shipped in the already-applied `20260609214356_InitialCreate` (line 68) that SDD-INV-001 §2.14 forbids editing. So `InvoiceOpenItem.BookingExchangeRate` mirrors the rate the invoice actually froze, NOT a placeholder. This spec therefore relies on a real booking rate for both `RealizedFxDifference` (§2.9) and SDD-PAY-003's `BaseOutstanding`.
- **Fallback is a contingency, not a design.** If the amendment slips, the projection MUST fall back to `DueDate := IssueDate` and `BookingExchangeRate := 1.000000`, and that fallback MUST be recorded in the change spec. It is a degraded path, not an equivalent one: a fabricated `1.000000` makes aging (SDD-PAY-003) understate due dates and misstates `RealizedFxDifference` and `BaseOutstanding` for every non-base-currency invoice. The fallback MUST be removed once the amendment lands.

### 2.3 Projection feed & consumer idempotency (MUST — SDD-INFRA-006 / SDD-INV-001)
- FOUR consumers MUST live in `src/Interfaces/Payments/Finance.Payments.API/Consumers/` — `InvoiceConfirmedEventConsumer`, `InvoicePostedEventConsumer`, `InvoiceCancelledEventConsumer`, `InvoiceReversedEventConsumer` — registered inside the `AddFinanceMessageBus<PaymentsDbContext>(configuration, ConfigureConsumers)` delegate.
- **Only SETTLEABLE document types enter the projection (MUST).** The `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` consumer MUST NOT create an open item for an invoice whose `DocumentType` no `SettlementPairing` entry can settle. The skip MUST be a SILENT SUCCESS — no row, no throw, no dead letter — and SHOULD emit one structured NLog debug/information record naming the invoice and its type (SDD-OBS-001, no string interpolation). The predicate MUST be `SettlementPairing.IsSettleableInvoiceType(InvoiceDocumentType)` (§2.5 rule 10), and it MUST be DERIVED from the same pair table as the allocation rule — computed as "some `PaymentDocumentType` satisfies `CanSettle(paymentType, documentType)`", NEVER re-listed as a second literal set — so the admission rule and the allocation rule can never drift. In v1 that excludes exactly ONE type, `CreditNote` (the pairs admit `SaleInvoice`, `DebitNote` and `PurchaseInvoice` only), and WIDENING the pair table (§5) automatically widens the projection with no second edit. The predicate is evaluable from the event alone with no cross-service read: `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` already carries `required InvoiceDocumentType DocumentType` (`src/Finance.ServiceModel/Events/Invoices/InvoiceConfirmedEvent.cs`).
  - **Why the skip is REQUIRED.** Without it a confirmed credit note would age forever as a phantom balance. `InvoiceDocumentTypeMap.DirectionFor(CreditNote)` returns `AP` (`src/Interfaces/Invoices/Finance.Invoices.API/Services/InvoiceDocumentTypeMap.cs`) while the note itself moves the CUSTOMER control account, and no payment document type can ever settle it (§2.5 rule 10) — so its `Outstanding` could never reach `0.00`, SDD-PAY-003 §2.1's only other exit is a terminal `InvoiceStatus` it never reaches, and a 200.00 credit note to customer C would be reported as an AP payable ageing 1-30 → 90+ forever while the AR sub-ledger read `0.00` against a non-zero GL customer control account. Both control accounts would diverge permanently.
  - **The skip excludes the note from MATCHING, not from accounting.** The credit note still exists, still confirms, still posts, and still moves the GL through SDD-INV-001; it is absent from the allocation and aging surface ONLY. Credit-note settlement (a refund, or an offset against the corrected invoice via `CorrectsInvoiceId`) is DEFERRED with the refund feature (§1, §5). Admitting it later is a `SettlementPairing` widening plus a one-off backfill of the skipped invoices — no schema change and no code change to the consumer, which reads the predicate.
  - **One residual consequence MUST be stated, not hidden.** `InvoicePostedEvent` (payload: `InvoiceId`, `JournalEntryId`, `JournalEntryNumber`) and the new `InvoiceReversedEvent` carry NO `DocumentType`, so neither consumer can tell a deliberately skipped invoice from a confirmation that has not landed yet. Their missing-row throw MUST therefore be retained exactly as specified below — it is what makes the out-of-order case converge (§2.14) and MUST NOT be downgraded to a silent success. The consequence is that a POSTED (or later reversed) non-settleable invoice exhausts the retry schedule and dead-letters on `<queue>_error`. That dead letter is EXPECTED and carries NO data loss — the projection is the allocation/aging surface and a `CreditNote` belongs to neither — so it MUST NOT be read as projection drift by operator triage or by the §7 reconciliation job. Eliminating it requires `DocumentType` on both events, an SDD-INV-001 payload change this batch does not own (§7 open item). `InvoiceCancelledEvent` carries no `DocumentType` either (payload: `InvoiceId`, nullable `DocumentNumber`, `Reason`), so a cancelled credit note MAY still tombstone a `Cancelled` row; that is harmless, because a `Cancelled` row is excluded from every allocation (§2.5 rule 3) and aging path.
- For a document type the admission rule accepts, `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` MUST upsert the open item keyed by `InvoiceId`: insert with `InvoiceStatus = Confirmed` and `SettledAmount = 0.00`, or refresh the externally-owned columns of an existing row — unless that row is already in a terminal status, which MUST be left untouched (see the terminal rule below). `LastAppliedAt` MUST be stamped on every applied event.
- `InvoicePostedEvent` MUST set `InvoiceStatus = Posted` on the existing row. It MUST NOT create a row — the event carries only `InvoiceId`/`JournalEntryId`/`JournalEntryNumber`, which is not enough to build a valid open item. A missing row MUST make the consumer throw so MassTransit retries (1s/5s/15s → `<queue>_error` DLQ, SDD-INFRA-006 §2.5 — a contract that only holds because CHG-FIX-006 ships in this batch, see the idempotency bullet below); a genuinely lost pair is repaired by the deferred reconciliation job (§7), never by inventing a partial row.
- **`InvoiceReversedEvent` MUST set `InvoiceStatus = Reversed`** on the existing row, which makes the item ineligible for further allocation (§2.5 rule 3). The event is NEW in this batch: `src/Finance.ServiceModel/Events/Invoices/InvoiceReversedEvent.cs`, a `sealed record` implementing `IFinanceEvent` with `required` properties plus `MessageId`/`CorrelationId`/`OccurredAt`, published through the outbox from the SDD-INV-001 §2.7 reversal path, which owns its full payload; this consumer requires only `InvoiceId`. Without it a reversed invoice — whose GL effect is fully offset — would read `Posted` in the projection forever and remain a legal allocation target, so a real receipt could be matched to a document carrying no ledger balance while the genuinely open invoice stayed outstanding. Like the posted consumer it MUST NOT create a row (a reversal presupposes a `Posted` invoice the projection has already seen); a missing row MUST make the consumer throw so MassTransit retries (same CHG-FIX-006 prerequisite as above). It MUST NOT delete the row and MUST NOT remove or release existing `PaymentAllocation` rows.
- `InvoiceCancelledEvent` MUST set `InvoiceStatus = Cancelled`, which makes the item ineligible for further allocation (§2.5). It MUST NOT delete the row and MUST NOT remove or release existing `PaymentAllocation` rows — history stays auditable (SDD-AUDIT-001).
- **A cancellation for an unknown `InvoiceId` MUST TOMBSTONE, not no-op.** When the `InvoiceId` is absent from the projection the consumer MUST UPSERT a row with `InvoiceStatus = Cancelled`, populating what the event carries (`InvoiceId`, `DocumentNumber` when non-null — it is `null` for a draft cancel, per the shipped `InvoiceCancelledEvent`) and leaving every unknown externally-owned column at its zero/default value (`0.00` amounts, `1.000000` rate, empty strings, `Guid.Empty` counterparty, the event's `OccurredAt` for both dates, `SettledAmount = 0.00`). A `Cancelled` row is excluded from every allocation (§2.5 rule 3) and aging (SDD-PAY-003) path, so those placeholder values are never read. The consumer MUST NOT throw — this remains the one deliberate exception to the "missing row ⇒ throw for retry" rule the posted and reversed consumers follow, because SDD-INV-001 §2.6 publishes the event for a `Draft` cancel too and a draft never enters the projection, so retrying could only DLQ every draft cancellation.
  - The tombstone exists because a plain no-op is not safe under retry. If the `InvoiceConfirmedEvent`'s first delivery fails transiently and is retried on the 1s/5s/15s schedule, an `InvoiceCancelledEvent` for the same invoice can land in the gap, find no row, and succeed — and the confirmation retry would then insert the row as `Confirmed`. The projection would show a cancelled invoice as `Confirmed` permanently: §2.5 would let an operator allocate real cash to it, SDD-PAY-003 would age it in every bucket and total, and the orphaned-settlement warning below would never fire because the cancellation saw no row.
- **`Cancelled` and `Reversed` are TERMINAL in the projection.** A consumer MUST NOT move a row out of either status: any event — in practice an `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` or `InvoicePostedEvent`, including a retry that lost the race to the tombstone above — whose row already reads `Cancelled` or `Reversed` MUST be a successful no-op that leaves the row untouched. This is what makes the tombstone effective, and it mirrors the invoice aggregate, where both statuses are terminal (`src/Finance.Common/Enums/InvoiceStatus.cs`).
- **Orphaned-settlement detection (MUST — the authoritative side of `INVOICE_HAS_SETTLEMENTS`).** When the open item ALREADY existed (a freshly tombstoned row cannot have allocations: §2.5 rule 2 requires a projection row before any allocation can be created), the consumer MUST also check whether any `PaymentAllocation` rows exist for that `InvoiceId` and, when any do, MUST emit a structured NLog **warning** (SDD-OBS-001 — no string interpolation) flagging an **orphaned settlement**: the invoice was cancelled while allocations still point at it. SDD-INV-001's `INVOICE_HAS_SETTLEMENTS` (409) cancel guard reads the invoice's own asynchronously-fed `SettledAmount` mirror and is therefore best-effort; the handshake is one-way with no synchronous read (§2.10), so a cancel racing an in-flight allocation can still get through. **Payments — not Invoices — is the authority for what is actually allocated**, so this warning is the detection point. The consumer MUST still apply the status change (it MUST NOT throw, MUST NOT delete rows, and MUST NOT auto-release the allocations, §2.14); repair is operator-driven deallocation or the deferred reconciliation job (§7).
- Each consumer MUST be wrapped by `IdempotencyFilter<T>` (Redis `SETNX finance:processed:{MessageId}`, 7-day TTL — SDD-INFRA-006). No consumer MUST hand-roll dedupe logic.
- **Every retry-then-dead-letter contract in this section (and in §2.14) depends on CHG-FIX-006, which ships in THIS batch.** Before that fix `IdempotencyFilter<T>.Send` claimed the `finance:processed:{MessageId}` key and called the downstream pipe with NO try/catch (`src/Infrastructure/Messaging/Finance.Infrastructure.Messaging/Filters/IdempotencyFilter.cs`), while `bus.UseMessageRetry(retry => retry.Intervals(RetryIntervals))` was registered BEFORE `bus.UseFinanceIdempotency(context)` (`src/Infrastructure/Messaging/Finance.Infrastructure.Messaging/MessagingServiceCollectionExtensions.cs:122-123`) — so retry was the OUTER filter: a throwing consumer's retry re-entered the filter, found the filter's OWN claim, logged "Duplicate message skipped", returned success, and the message was ACKed instead of ever reaching `<queue>_error`. CHG-FIX-006 makes the filter DELETE the claim and rethrow when the downstream pipe throws, so the 1s/5s/15s schedule and the DLQ actually run. CHG-FIX-006 is therefore a hard PREREQUISITE of every "throw so MassTransit retries" clause in this spec; none of them MUST be implemented against a build that lacks it.
- Beyond `MessageId` dedupe, each consumer MUST be a convergent UPSERT keyed by `InvoiceId` so a post-TTL replay, a DLQ redelivery, or an out-of-order pair still converges. In particular a late `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` MUST NOT downgrade an already-`Posted` open item, and MUST NOT resurrect a `Cancelled` or `Reversed` one (the terminal rule above) — status MUST only move `Confirmed → Posted` and into a terminal state, never back.
- **Eventual consistency (MUST be surfaced, not smoothed over).** The projection lags its source. An `InvoiceId` absent from the projection MUST yield `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` (404) and the caller SHOULD retry; the service MUST NOT fall back to a synchronous Refit read against the Invoices service on the allocation path, and MUST NOT create a speculative open item from the allocation request. Absence now has TWO causes — the lag, which clears, and the admission rule above, which never does for a non-settleable type — and the service MUST NOT try to tell them apart (that would need the very cross-service read this projection exists to remove); the distinction is a documentation and frontend-copy obligation only (§4).
- A nightly reconciliation job that re-reads confirmed/posted invoices and repairs drifted or missing open items MAY be added. It is DEFERRED (§7); when built it MUST be idempotent, MUST NOT write `SettledAmount`, MUST apply the SAME admission predicate (so it never re-creates a row the confirmed consumer deliberately skipped), and MUST NOT be on the request path.

### 2.4 Allocate — `POST /api/v1/payments/{id}/allocations` (MUST)
- The endpoint MUST accept an `AllocatePaymentRequest` carrying an EXPLICIT list of `{ InvoiceId, AllocatedAmount }` items plus the payment's base64 `RowVersion`, and MUST create one `PaymentAllocation` row per item. Requires `finance.payment:allocate`.
- v1 MUST require the explicit list. Automatic FIFO / oldest-due-first matching MUST NOT be implied by an omitted list (an empty list MUST be rejected with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_ITEMS_REQUIRED`); auto-matching is DEFERRED (§5/§7).
- The request MUST be all-or-nothing: one transaction over all items. If any item fails any chain validator, NO row is written, NO event is published, NO audit row is created, and `Payment.AllocatedAmount` is unchanged.
- On success the service MUST, within a single SaveChanges/outbox transaction and in this order:
  1. Load the `Payment` tracked with its `Allocations`; a missing payment MUST yield `PAYMENT_NOT_FOUND`.
  2. Apply the client `RowVersion` as the concurrency `OriginalValue`; a malformed base64 token MUST yield `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION`.
  3. Load the `InvoiceOpenItem` rows for the requested `InvoiceId` set (one tracked query, not one per item) and build the `PaymentAllocationValidationContext`.
  4. Run the §2.5 chain in the documented order, short-circuiting on the first failure.
  5. Insert one `PaymentAllocation` per item, computing `BaseAllocatedAmount` and `RealizedFxDifference` (§2.9) and stamping `AllocatedAt`, `AllocatedBy`, `CorrelationId`.
     - **5b.** Invoke `IRealizedFxHandler.HandleAsync(context, cancellationToken)` once per allocation row inside the same transaction; a non-success `Result` MUST fail the whole allocation (the registered `NoOpRealizedFxHandler` always succeeds in v1, §2.9). The seam MUST actually be called on this path — computing and storing the column without invoking the handler would ship the only SDD-FIN-005 extension point unwired.
  6. Increase `Payment.AllocatedAmount` by the sum of the new rows (`UnallocatedAmount` is computed, never stored — SDD-PAY-001).
  7. Increase each `InvoiceOpenItem.SettledAmount` by its item's amount and recompute the derived `SettlementStatus` (§2.8).
  8. Write one audit `Update` row per created allocation (`EventType = "PaymentAllocated"`) **before** the outbox rows (audit-first, SDD-AUDIT-001 — §2.11).
  9. Enqueue one `PaymentAllocatedEvent` per created allocation to the outbox (atomic with the DB write; no `await _bus.Publish` outside the outbox, no try/catch — SDD-INFRA-006). Each event's `OccurredAt` MUST be stamped HERE, INSIDE this transaction (§2.10): it is the ordering token SDD-INV-001 §2.14/§2.15 compares against the invoice's `LastSettlementAppliedAt`, and it MUST NOT be re-stamped when the outbox dispatches or publishes the row.
  10. `SaveWithConcurrencyCheckAsync` then commit the transaction; the payment's `RowVersion` increments.
- Allocation MUST NOT create, mutate, or reverse a journal entry, MUST NOT change `Payment.Status`, and MUST NOT call `IWorkflowEngine<Payment>` — it is not a lifecycle transition (§1 principle 1).
- The response MUST be an `AllocatePaymentResultDto` carrying the created `PaymentAllocationDto` rows, the payment's new `AllocatedAmount` and computed `UnallocatedAmount`, its new `RowVersion`, and each affected invoice's `SettledAmount` + derived `SettlementStatus`, so the caller needs no follow-up read.

### 2.5 Allocation invariant chain (MUST — SDD-INFRA-007)
- Shape-only rules stay in FluentValidation (§3.1). Every rule below depends on other rows or on aggregate state, so each MUST be an `IChainValidator<PaymentAllocationValidationContext>` in `src/Interfaces/Payments/Finance.Payments.API/Validation/` (one class per rule, one file each — mirroring the Journal `Validation/` folder), registered in this exact order via `services.AddValidationChain<PaymentAllocationValidationContext>(...)`. The chain short-circuits, and the service MUST return `Result<T>.Failure(result.ErrorCode!, result.Detail)`:
  1. `PaymentAllocatableValidator` — `Payment.Status` MUST be `Confirmed` or `Posted` → `PAYMENT_NOT_ALLOCATABLE`. A `Draft` payment is not yet a legally-numbered document; a `Cancelled`/`Reversed` payment MUST NOT be matched.
  2. `AllocationInvoiceKnownValidator` — every requested `InvoiceId` MUST exist in the local projection → `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND`.
  3. `AllocationInvoiceEligibleValidator` — each `InvoiceOpenItem.InvoiceStatus` MUST be `Confirmed` or `Posted`, and MUST NOT be `Cancelled` or `Reversed` → `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`. The whitelist is exhaustive over the §2.2 value set: both terminal statuses are rejected, `Reversed` included, because a reversed invoice's GL effect is fully offset and matching real cash to it would leave the genuinely open invoice outstanding. The rule is only ENFORCEABLE because the §2.3 `InvoiceReversedEventConsumer` mirrors the reversal — a projection that never learned about reversal would keep reading `Posted` and pass this rule.
  4. `AllocationDirectionValidator` — the payment `Direction` MUST equal the invoice `Direction` → `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_DIRECTION_MISMATCH`. The comparison MUST be by enum member NAME (`AR`/`AP`): `PaymentDirection` (SDD-PAY-001) and `InvoiceDirection` (SDD-INV-001) are distinct CLR enums with identical members, and the projection stores the invoice value as a string. This rule is a CHEAP PRE-FILTER on CASH direction and is NOT by itself an accounting match: the shipped `InvoiceDirection` groups `SaleInvoice` with `DebitNote` under `AR` and `PurchaseInvoice` with `CreditNote` under `AP` (`src/Finance.Common/Enums/InvoiceDirection.cs`; `InvoiceDocumentTypeMap.DirectionFor` in `src/Interfaces/Invoices/Finance.Invoices.API/Services/`), so equal directions do NOT imply the two documents moved the same control account. Rule 10 states the accounting match explicitly (although §2.3's admission rule makes it unreachable in v1 — see the note after rule 10, which is why this rule is the one that actually fires); this rule is retained because it is also the cheaper, more specific diagnostic for the common operator error.
  5. `AllocationCounterpartyValidator` — `Payment.CounterpartyId` MUST equal `InvoiceOpenItem.CounterpartyId` → `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_COUNTERPARTY_MISMATCH`. No Warehouse read is performed; both values are opaque GUIDs.
  6. `AllocationCurrencyValidator` — `Payment.CurrencyCode` MUST equal `InvoiceOpenItem.CurrencyCode` → `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_CURRENCY_MISMATCH`. v1 REQUIRES equality even when the two documents' own base amounts would reconcile; cross-currency allocation is DEFERRED to SDD-FIN-005.
  7. `AllocationDuplicateValidator` — no `PaymentAllocation` MUST already exist for the `(PaymentId, InvoiceId)` pair, AND no pair MUST appear twice WITHIN the request → `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_DUPLICATE`. An existing match is changed by deallocating and re-allocating (§2.6); v1 has no in-place amount amendment.
  8. `AllocationWithinPaymentValidator` — `Σ(existing allocations) + Σ(requested items)` MUST be `≤ Payment.Amount` → `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_PAYMENT`. The validator MUST sum the request items together, NOT validate each item independently.
  9. `AllocationWithinOutstandingValidator` — per invoice, `InvoiceOpenItem.SettledAmount + requested` MUST be `≤ InvoiceOpenItem.GrossTotal` → `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_OUTSTANDING`.
  10. `AllocationControlAccountValidator` — the pair `(Payment.DocumentType, InvoiceOpenItem.DocumentType)` MUST be a documented settlement pair → `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_CONTROL_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH`. This is the rule that makes a match an ACCOUNTING match: the payment's posting rule is chosen solely from its own `DocumentType` and is fixed in the GL at confirm (SDD-PAY-001 §2.13), so unless the invoice's document type moved the SAME control account, allocating drifts both control accounts from their sub-ledgers by the allocated amount. The permitted pairs are exactly:
     - `CustomerReceipt` → `{ SaleInvoice, DebitNote }` (customer control account)
     - `SupplierPayment` → `{ PurchaseInvoice }` (supplier control account)
     Every other combination MUST be rejected. The pair set MUST be owned by ONE shared table — `SettlementPairing` in `src/Finance.Common/Settlement/SettlementPairing.cs`, exposing `AllocatableInvoiceTypesFor(PaymentDocumentType)`, `CanSettle(PaymentDocumentType, InvoiceDocumentType)` and `IsSettleableInvoiceType(InvoiceDocumentType)` — so no service re-derives it and the two-valued `PaymentDocumentType` (SDD-PAY-001) and four-valued `InvoiceDocumentType` are related in exactly one place. `IsSettleableInvoiceType` MUST be DERIVED from `CanSettle` over every `PaymentDocumentType` and MUST NOT be a second literal list: it is the §2.3 projection-admission predicate, and deriving it from the same pairs is what makes admission and allocation incapable of drifting.
- **Why the rule pairs DOCUMENT TYPES rather than resolving account codes.** The verified shipped rules tie only two pairs by construction: `SALE_INVOICE` debits `411` and `PAYMENT_CUSTOMER_RECEIPT` credits `411`; `PURCHASE_INVOICE` credits `401` and `PAYMENT_SUPPLIER_PAYMENT` debits `401` (`src/Country/Finance.Country.BG/BulgariaStrategy.cs`; SDD-PAY-001 §2.13). `BulgariaStrategy.BuildDefaultRules()` ships exactly three templates (`SALE_INVOICE`, `PURCHASE_INVOICE`, `CUSTOMER_PAYMENT`) — there is NO `CREDIT_NOTE` or `DEBIT_NOTE` rule in code, so a credit note's control account cannot be read off shipped behavior, and one `CreditNote` document type with one `CREDIT_NOTE` rule key cannot express both the customer (`411`) and supplier (`401`) case; deciding it needs accountant input, which is exactly what the SDD-PAY-001 §7 sign-off gate covers. A code-level `ControlAccountFor(...)` map in `Finance.Common` would additionally put Bulgarian NCA account codes into country-agnostic core, which SDD-CTRY-001 / CLAUDE.md §0.3 forbid — account selectors belong to `ICountryStrategy`. Pairing document types states the same invariant, is country-agnostic, and needs no accountant input for the pairs that provably tie. It MUST live in `Finance.Common` for a third, mechanical reason: the existing per-type discriminator map `InvoiceDocumentTypeMap` is internal to `Finance.Invoices.API` (`src/Interfaces/Invoices/Finance.Invoices.API/Services/`), and `Finance.Payments.API` MUST NOT project-reference another service's API (§7), so the pairing cannot be hosted there. `DebitNote` is admitted because `DirectionFor(DebitNote) = AR` and `AR` IS the customer control account on both sides of that pair (`SALE_INVOICE` debits `411`; `PAYMENT_CUSTOMER_RECEIPT` credits `411`), and because the SDD-PAY-001 §2.13 `DEBIT_NOTE` template repeats the `SALE_INVOICE` sides. The direction→control-account inference fails ONLY for `CreditNote`: its `AP` classification describes the CASH direction of a note issued to a customer, while the note itself moves the CUSTOMER control account — which is exactly why it cannot be admitted without accountant input. If the `DEBIT_NOTE` template's accounts change at sign-off, `SettlementPairing` is the single place to revisit (§7). Credit-note settlement is DEFERRED with the refund/offset feature (§1, §5).
- **Rule 10 is UNREACHABLE through the v1 paths and is retained as defense-in-depth** — the same label §2.6 applies to the `Cancelled`/`Reversed` deallocation branch and SDD-PAY-001 applies to `PAYMENT_BASE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`. It is registered TENTH, so a request that breaks BOTH direction and pairing short-circuits on the more specific `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_DIRECTION_MISMATCH`. Once §2.3 keeps every non-settleable type out of the projection, the only document types an eligible open item can carry are `SaleInvoice` and `DebitNote` (both `AR`) and `PurchaseInvoice` (`AP`) — so every pair rule 10 would reject also breaks the direction rule and short-circuits six rules earlier. The one same-direction mismatch the direction rule cannot see, a `SupplierPayment` against a customer `CreditNote` (both `AP`), can no longer be REQUESTED at all: the credit note has no open item, so rule 2 rejects it with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` (404) eight rules earlier. Rule 10, its error code, its §3.2/§3.3 rows and its §2.14 edge case MUST still be implemented and tested — against a directly-seeded open item, since no consumer can produce one — because the rule becomes REACHABLE the moment `SettlementPairing` is widened (§5) or a future path admits a document type whose control account the direction rule cannot separate, and because it is the only rule that states the accounting invariant explicitly.
- Every comparison MUST be an exact `decimal` comparison at 2 decimal places — no epsilon, no tolerance band, no rounding slack. A single cent over the bound MUST fail (§2.14).
- The chain MUST run against the SAME tracked `DbContext` instance and inside the SAME transaction that performs the write, so the amounts it sums are the amounts persisted. The payment `RowVersion` and the open-item `RowVersion` are the final race guards (§2.14).
- The chain MUST be exercised by the deallocate path only for its state rules (rule 1); rules 2–10 are allocation-only (releasing an amount can never breach an upper bound, and a match that was legal when created stays releasable even after the invoice reaches a terminal status).

### 2.6 Deallocate — `DELETE /api/v1/payments/{id}/allocations/{allocationId}` (MUST)
- The endpoint MUST remove exactly one allocation row and release the matched amount. Requires `finance.payment:allocate`. It MAY accept the payment's base64 `RowVersion` as an OPTIONAL `rowVersion` query parameter; when supplied it MUST be applied as the concurrency token, and when omitted the tracked token loaded inside the transaction still guards a concurrent write (`CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION`).
- The lookup MUST be scoped by `(PaymentId, Id)`. An `allocationId` that exists but belongs to a DIFFERENT payment MUST yield `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_NOT_FOUND` (404) — never a cross-payment delete.
- The payment MUST be `Confirmed` or `Posted` → otherwise `PAYMENT_NOT_ALLOCATABLE`. A `Cancelled`/`Reversed` payment's allocation rows MUST be left intact for audit and MUST NOT be removable through this endpoint. That clause is **defensive code**: SDD-PAY-001 hard-blocks BOTH cancel (§2.6) and reverse (§2.7, §3.2) while `AllocatedAmount > 0` with `PAYMENT_HAS_ALLOCATIONS`, so an allocated payment can never reach `Cancelled`/`Reversed` and this branch is unreachable through the v1 paths — it is retained as defense-in-depth against a future path that voids a payment without checking (exactly as SDD-PAY-001 retains `PAYMENT_BASE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`). Release-on-reverse is **RESOLVED, not deferred**: allocations are NEVER auto-released and the operator MUST deallocate before cancelling or reversing (SDD-PAY-001 §2.7; §7 Resolved).
- On success the service MUST, within a single SaveChanges/outbox transaction and in this order:
  1. Load the payment and the allocation row; serialize the `BeforeJson` snapshot (the payment's pre-change matching projection plus the row being removed — §2.11).
  2. Decrease `Payment.AllocatedAmount` by the row's `AllocatedAmount`.
  3. Decrease the `InvoiceOpenItem.SettledAmount` by the same amount and recompute `SettlementStatus` (§2.8).
  4. Write the audit `Update` row (`EventType = "PaymentDeallocated"`, non-empty `BeforeJson`) **before** the outbox row.
  5. Enqueue `PaymentDeallocatedEvent` to the outbox, with `OccurredAt` stamped INSIDE this transaction (§2.10) — a release whose token looks older than the allocation it reverses would be DROPPED by the SDD-INV-001 §2.15 mirror.
  6. Remove the allocation row, `SaveWithConcurrencyCheckAsync`, commit.
- Deallocation MUST NOT post, reverse, or touch a journal entry, and MUST NOT change `Payment.Status` — removing a match is not a ledger event (§1 principle 1).
- Neither decrement MUST be able to drive `Payment.AllocatedAmount` or `InvoiceOpenItem.SettledAmount` below `0.00`; the invariant holds by construction (every decrement releases an amount that was previously added by the same code path) and MUST be asserted by test.

### 2.7 List allocations — `GET /api/v1/payments/{id}/allocations` (MUST)
- The endpoint MUST accept a `FilterRequest` and return `PagedResult<PaymentAllocationDto>` (SDD-INFRA-005), default-ordered by `AllocatedAt` descending then `Id` (the PK appended as the final deterministic sort term). `PageSize` MUST be capped at 200. Requires `finance.payment:read`.
- Each row MUST be enriched with the invoice's `DocumentNumber`, `DueDate`, `InvoiceStatus`, `GrossTotal` and derived `SettlementStatus` joined from the LOCAL `InvoiceOpenItem` table (a same-database join, which is permitted). No cross-service read MUST occur on this path.
- An unknown payment MUST yield `PAYMENT_NOT_FOUND` (404). A payment with no allocations MUST return an empty page with `200` — an unallocated payment is a normal business state, not an error.
- The list MUST NOT be cached (§2.13).

### 2.8 Derived settlement state (MUST)
- `SettlementStatus` MUST be a NEW enum in `src/Finance.Common/Enums/SettlementStatus.cs` with explicit values `Unsettled = 1`, `PartiallySettled = 2`, `Settled = 3`.
- **This spec is the SINGLE owner of that enum, and there MUST be exactly ONE settlement enum in the solution.** It lives in `Finance.Common` precisely so both services share it: the SDD-INV-001 amendment MUST bind its `Invoice.SettlementStatus` property to this same shared enum and MUST NOT declare a parallel `InvoiceSettlementStatus` type, and SDD-PAY-003 MUST NOT declare one either. A second enum with identical members would force a name-based translation across the boundary for no benefit (contrast `PaymentDirection`/`InvoiceDirection`, §2.5 rule 4, where two aggregate-owned enums already exist and the string comparison is unavoidable).
- It MUST be **derived**, never stored as an independent workflow state and never transitioned through `IWorkflowEngine<T>` (SDD-INFRA-008 governs aggregates with real lifecycles; settlement is a pure function of two decimals). The derivation MUST live in one place — `Services/SettlementStatusCalculator.cs` — and MUST NOT be duplicated in the DTO mapper, the events, or SDD-PAY-003.
- Derivation, by EXACT `DECIMAL(18,2)` comparison of `SettledAmount` against `GrossTotal`:
  - `SettledAmount == 0.00` → `Unsettled`.
  - `0.00 < SettledAmount < GrossTotal` → `PartiallySettled`.
  - `SettledAmount ≥ GrossTotal` → `Settled` (the `>` branch is defensive only — over-allocation is forbidden by §2.5 so `>` MUST be unreachable).
- There MUST be no tolerance band and no automatic write-off: `GrossTotal − 0.01` settled MUST be `PartiallySettled`, not `Settled`. A tolerance/write-off rule MAY be introduced later and would be a BREAKING change to this derivation (§5).
- The payment side has no `SettlementStatus`: a payment is fully applied when its computed `UnallocatedAmount == 0.00` (SDD-PAY-001). The two MUST NOT be conflated.
- The derived value MUST be recomputed after every allocate/deallocate, carried on the DTOs (§2.4/§2.7) and stamped onto the events (§2.10) so the Invoices-side consumer does not have to reproduce the rule across the boundary.

### 2.9 Realized-FX difference seam (MUST — dormant, deferred to SDD-FIN-005)
- Because allocation REQUIRES payment currency == invoice currency (§2.5), no transactional-currency conversion happens while matching. A **base-currency** difference still arises whenever `Payment.ExchangeRate ≠ InvoiceOpenItem.BookingExchangeRate`: the two DOCUMENTS froze different rates for the same transactional amount, so their own recorded base values disagree for the allocated portion.
- **The difference is a document-level figure, not a ledger figure.** It MUST NOT be described as reconciling two GL base balances, and no requirement here or in SDD-PAY-001 depends on the ledger holding rate-converted base amounts. Verified: the shipped `PostingEngine.BuildLine` (`src/Interfaces/Journal/Finance.Journal.API/Services/PostingEngine.cs`) pins every posted line to `ExchangeRate = 1.000000m` and writes the transactional amount straight into `BaseDebitAmount`/`BaseCreditAmount`, and `CheckBalanced` compares only those base columns — so for EVERY document type, invoices included, the general ledger currently stores the transactional amount in its base-amount columns. That is a pre-existing SDD-FIN-006 limitation owned by the not-yet-authored SDD-FIN-005 (recorded as a known limitation by SDD-PAY-001 and SDD-PAY-003); this spec MUST NOT attempt to work around it, and MUST NOT assert that the GL is multi-currency-correct. The two inputs to the computation are the aggregates' STORED rates (`Payment.ExchangeRate`, the invoice's frozen `ExchangeRate` mirrored as `InvoiceOpenItem.BookingExchangeRate`), never a journal-entry line.
- The service MUST compute, per allocation row, `RealizedFxDifference = round(AllocatedAmount × (Payment.ExchangeRate − InvoiceOpenItem.BookingExchangeRate))`, rounded through `ICountryStrategy` (the country owns the monetary 2-decimal rounding entry point — the core MUST NOT inline a rounding mode; SDD-CTRY-001 / SDD-FIN-005). All arithmetic MUST be `decimal`.
- The value MUST be stored SIGNED on the row: `> 0` means the payment's own recorded base value exceeded the invoice's recorded base value for the allocated portion, `< 0` the converse (both are document-level values at the aggregates' frozen rates, not GL amounts). The gain-versus-loss INTERPRETATION per `Direction` (`AR`/`AP`) is owned by the eventual posting rule (SDD-FIN-005), NOT by this spec, and MUST NOT be baked into the column.
- The difference MUST be handed to an `IRealizedFxHandler` seam (`Task<Result> HandleAsync(RealizedFxContext context, CancellationToken cancellationToken)`) invoked inside the allocation transaction, and a default `NoOpRealizedFxHandler` returning `Result.Success()` for every input MUST be registered so allocation works end-to-end while SDD-FIN-005 is unauthored. This is exactly the seam shape SDD-FIN-002 §2.7 used for `IPostingPeriodGuard` + `AlwaysOpenPostingPeriodGuard`.
- Until SDD-FIN-005 lands the difference is **informational only**: it MUST NOT produce a journal entry, MUST NOT change any GL or trial-balance figure (SDD-FIN-003), and MUST NOT be netted into `AllocatedAmount`, `BaseAllocatedAmount`, `SettledAmount`, or the `SettlementStatus` derivation. It MAY be surfaced on `PaymentAllocationDto` and MUST be carried on `PaymentAllocatedEvent`.
- The seam MUST be invoked once per allocation row (§2.4 step 5b) even when the computed difference is `0.00` — the invocation is the contract, not the value. The difference IS exactly `0.00` whenever `Payment.ExchangeRate` equals the mirrored `BookingExchangeRate`, which is the common path, so the registered no-op keeps allocation inert in practice. Because `BookingExchangeRate` mirrors the invoice's REAL frozen `ExchangeRate` (§2.2), the difference is a genuine figure for every currency and MUST NOT be reasoned about as though the booking rate were always `1.000000`.

### 2.10 Domain events & the settlement handshake with Invoices (MUST — SDD-INFRA-006)
- `PaymentAllocatedEvent` and `PaymentDeallocatedEvent` MUST be `sealed record` types implementing `IFinanceEvent` in `src/Finance.ServiceModel/Events/Payments/`, with `required` properties plus `MessageId`, `CorrelationId`, `OccurredAt`, published through the EF transactional outbox configured on `PaymentsDbContext` ONLY.
- `PaymentAllocatedEvent` MUST carry: `MessageId`, `CorrelationId`, `OccurredAt`, `PaymentId`, `DocumentNumber`, `InvoiceId`, `Direction`, `CounterpartyId`, `CurrencyCode`, `AllocatedAmount`, `BaseAllocatedAmount`, `RealizedFxDifference`, `InvoiceSettledAmount`, `InvoiceSettlementStatus`, `AllocatedAt`.
- `PaymentDeallocatedEvent` MUST carry: `MessageId`, `CorrelationId`, `OccurredAt`, `PaymentId`, `DocumentNumber`, `InvoiceId`, `ReleasedAmount`, `BaseReleasedAmount`, `InvoiceSettledAmount`, `InvoiceSettlementStatus`, `DeallocatedAt`.
- **`OccurredAt` is an ORDERING TOKEN, and its stamping point is part of this spec's contract (MUST).** Both events' `OccurredAt` MUST be stamped INSIDE the allocation/deallocation transaction (§2.4 step 9, §2.6 step 5) from the server clock, and MUST NOT be re-stamped when the outbox dispatches the row, when MassTransit publishes it, or when a consumer receives it. This obligation MUST be stated HERE, on the side that OWNS both events, not only on the consuming side: `src/Finance.ServiceModel/Events/IFinanceEvent.cs` makes the property mandatory on every Finance event but imposes NO transaction-boundary constraint, so nothing in the wire format enforces it. SDD-INV-001 §2.14/§2.15 compares the token against the invoice's `LastSettlementAppliedAt` and DROPS — silently and successfully — any event that is strictly older, which is what makes the invoice-side settlement mirror last-writer-by-`OccurredAt` rather than last-writer-by-arrival. A token stamped at dispatch or publish time would reorder concurrent allocations of the SAME invoice and leave that mirror permanently wrong (the SDD-INV-001 §2.14 worked example: 300.00 applied after 1000.00). Nothing is added to the wire format by this rule — only the stamping point is pinned.
- The payment's own number MUST be named `DocumentNumber` on both events — the aggregate's column name, and the name every other record in that folder that carries the payment's number uses (`PaymentConfirmedEvent`, `PaymentCancelledEvent`, `PaymentReversedEvent`; `PaymentPostedEvent` carries none — SDD-PAY-001 §2.14). The folder itself is created by this batch (`src/Finance.ServiceModel/Events/Payments/` does not exist in the repo today), so this is the convention the batch establishes, not one it inherits. `PaymentNumber` MUST NOT be introduced: the `<Entity>Number` form is reserved for CROSS-document references, such as `JournalEntryNumber` on the shipped `InvoicePostedEvent`. `InvoiceSettlementStatus` is an event PROPERTY name carrying a value of the ONE shared `SettlementStatus` enum (§2.8) — it MUST NOT be read as a second enum type.
- Exactly ONE event MUST be published per allocation row (never one aggregate event per multi-item request), so the Invoices-side consumer stays per-invoice, small, and idempotent.
- **The handshake is one-way.** The Invoices service consumes these events idempotently and updates the invoice's own `SettledAmount`/`SettlementStatus`; that invoice-side behavior is owned by the SDD-INV-001 amendment and MUST NOT be duplicated, re-specified, or second-guessed here. There MUST be NO back-event and allocation MUST NOT wait for, poll, or depend on the Invoices service — the Payments-local projection is the authority for the allocation invariants (§2.2/§2.5). This deliberately differs from SDD-PAY-001's posting handshake, which DOES use a dedicated back-event because the `JournalEntryId` has to come back.
- Both events MUST carry the ambient `CorrelationId` (SDD-INFRA-001) and are archived by the EventLog with no extra work (SDD-EVTLOG-001).

### 2.11 Audit trail (MUST — SDD-AUDIT-001)
- Every allocate and every deallocate MUST write an `audit.OperationsEvents` row in the SAME transaction as the data change and BEFORE the outbox row (audit-first).
- `EventType` MUST be `PaymentAllocated` / `PaymentDeallocated`, declared as constants in `src/Interfaces/Payments/Finance.Payments.API/Auditing/PaymentAuditEventTypes.cs` (which also declares `const string EntityType = "Payment"`).
- `Operation` MUST be `AuditOperation.Update` with `EntityType = "Payment"` and `EntityId = payment.Id.ToString()`: the audited subject is the payment whose matching changed.
- **The snapshot subject is the PAYMENT's matching state, never the allocation row alone.** `AuditService.RecordAsync` THROWS `ArgumentException` for an `Update` whose `BeforeJson` is null or empty, and on ALLOCATE the allocation row is being CREATED — so "the row before the change" does not exist and MUST NOT be used as the snapshot. Consistent with `EntityType = "Payment"` / `EntityId = payment.Id`:
  - On **allocate**, `BeforeJson` MUST be the payment's PRE-change matching snapshot — its `AllocatedAmount`, its computed `UnallocatedAmount`, and the existing `(InvoiceId, AllocatedAmount)` pairs (an empty pair list is still a non-empty JSON object, so the invariant holds for a first-ever allocation) — and `AfterJson` MUST be the SAME projection recomputed after the new rows.
  - On **deallocate**, `BeforeJson` MUST be that same payment projection plus the removed row's own snapshot, and `AfterJson` the projection after removal.
  - Both MUST be non-empty for every operation, so no allocate or deallocate can ever throw on the audit write.
- `UserId`/`Username` MUST come from `ICurrentUserAccessor`, `CorrelationId` from `ICorrelationIdAccessor`, `OccurredAt` server-side.
- One audit row MUST be written per allocation row created or removed (the batch-row alternative is recorded as an open item, §7).
- Allocate/deallocate are NOT on SDD-AUDIT-001's sensitive-operation list, so no `Reason` is REQUIRED. A caller MAY supply an optional free-text reason on deallocate; when supplied it MUST be persisted on the audit row.
- The `audit` schema's DENY-UPDATE/DELETE hardening is inherited from SDD-AUDIT-001 and is still unimplemented repo-wide (§7) — this spec MUST NOT work around it with application-level enforcement.

### 2.14 Edge cases (MUST)
- **Allocating against an invoice the projection has not seen yet.** MUST return `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` (404) — a legitimate transient outcome of the §2.3 lag; the caller SHOULD retry. MUST NOT trigger a synchronous read-through and MUST NOT create a speculative open item.
- **Exact-to-the-cent full settlement.** `SettledAmount == GrossTotal` exactly MUST be `Settled`; `GrossTotal − 0.01` MUST be `PartiallySettled`. No tolerance band (§2.8).
- **One cent over the outstanding.** A request that would make `SettledAmount = GrossTotal + 0.01` MUST fail with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_OUTSTANDING` and write nothing.
- **Multi-item request that over-allocates the payment.** Each item individually within its invoice's outstanding but the item SUM exceeding `Payment.Amount` MUST fail with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_PAYMENT` — the chain MUST sum the request, not validate item-by-item.
- **Duplicate invoice inside one request.** Two items for the same `InvoiceId` MUST fail with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_DUPLICATE` from the chain, NOT only from the unique index (a `DbUpdateException` MUST NOT be the user-facing path).
- **Concurrent allocation of the same payment.** Two simultaneous allocate calls — one MUST win; the other MUST fail with `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION`. The payment row is the serialization point for the `Σ ≤ Amount` invariant.
- **Concurrent allocation of the same invoice from two different payments.** Both chains may pass against the same `SettledAmount`; the second commit MUST fail with `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION` via the `InvoiceOpenItem.RowVersion` token. This is why the projection carries a `RowVersion` (§2.2).
- **Projection consumer writes during an allocation.** A §2.3 consumer applying an invoice event mid-allocation bumps `InvoiceOpenItem.RowVersion` and MUST surface as `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION` (409) — a benign, retryable outcome, not a data defect. An ordinary `InvoicePostedEvent` landing between §2.4 step 3 and step 10 can therefore fail a single, otherwise UNCONTENDED allocation; the operator simply retries and the second attempt sees the applied event. The projection MUST NOT drop its `RowVersion` to avoid this: losing it would reopen the two-payments race above.
- **Replayed invoice event.** A duplicate `InvoiceConfirmedEvent`/`InvoicePostedEvent`/`InvoiceCancelledEvent`/`InvoiceReversedEvent` MUST be a no-op — never a second open-item row, never a `SettledAmount` reset, never a status downgrade (§2.3).
- **Out-of-order invoice events.** `InvoicePostedEvent` (or `InvoiceReversedEvent`) arriving before `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` MUST NOT create a partial open item; the consumer MUST throw so MassTransit retries and finally DLQs (a recovery contract that holds only because CHG-FIX-006 ships in this batch — §2.3). A late `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` MUST NOT downgrade an already-`Posted` open item.
- **A confirmed `CreditNote` — a document type no payment can settle.** The `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` consumer MUST succeed WITHOUT creating an open item (§2.3, the `SettlementPairing.IsSettleableInvoiceType` predicate): no row, no throw, no dead letter. The note is therefore neither allocatable nor (SDD-PAY-003) aged, which is the point — a credit note that no payment can settle would otherwise age as a phantom AP balance forever. A later allocate attempt against it MUST fail at chain rule 2 with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` (404), NOT with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_CONTROL_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH`. Its subsequent `InvoicePostedEvent` finds no row and dead-letters as §2.3 documents — expected, no data loss, and NOT projection drift.
- **Cancellation delivered before the confirmation it races (tombstone).** An `InvoiceCancelledEvent` that arrives while the `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` is still being retried MUST tombstone a `Cancelled` row (§2.3), and the confirmation retry that lands afterwards MUST leave that row `Cancelled`. The invoice MUST NOT end up readable as `Confirmed`: were it to, §2.5 would accept real cash against a cancelled document and SDD-PAY-003 would age it in every bucket and total.
- **Allocating against a reversed invoice.** An open item whose `InvoiceStatus` is `Reversed` MUST fail with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (§2.5 rule 3) — a reversed invoice's ledger effect is fully offset, so matching cash to it would consume the payment's `UnallocatedAmount` while the genuinely open invoice stayed outstanding.
- **Same direction, different control account (UNREACHABLE — defense-in-depth).** A `SupplierPayment` (posting `401`/`503`) allocated to a customer `CreditNote` passes the direction rule (both are `AP`) and MAY pass the counterparty rule, but MUST fail with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_CONTROL_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH` (§2.5 rule 10) — the two documents moved DIFFERENT control accounts, and allocating would drift both from their sub-ledgers by the allocated amount. This state cannot be reached through the v1 paths, because §2.3 never projects a `CreditNote` and rule 2 therefore rejects the request as `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` first; the test MUST seed the open item DIRECTLY, and the rule is retained because widening `SettlementPairing` (§5) makes it reachable.
- **Invoice cancelled after being allocated (orphaned settlement).** The existing allocation rows MUST be left intact and MUST NOT be auto-released; further allocation MUST fail with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`. Because SDD-INV-001's `INVOICE_HAS_SETTLEMENTS` guard reads an eventually-consistent mirror, a cancel racing an in-flight allocation CAN reach this state; the consumer MUST detect it and emit the structured orphaned-settlement warning (§2.3), and the orphan MUST be repairable by operator deallocation or by the deferred reconciliation job (§7).
- **Cancellation of a `Draft` invoice the projection never saw.** An `InvoiceCancelledEvent` for an `InvoiceId` with no open-item row MUST succeed by TOMBSTONING a `Cancelled` row with default financial values (§2.3): the consumer MUST NOT throw. Treating it as the missing-row error would DLQ every draft cancellation; treating it as a plain no-op would leave the invoice resurrectable by a confirmation retry.
- **Allocating a `Draft` payment.** MUST fail with `PAYMENT_NOT_ALLOCATABLE` before any projection read.
- **Deallocating an allocation owned by another payment.** MUST return `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_NOT_FOUND` (404); the lookup MUST be scoped by `(PaymentId, Id)` (§2.6).
- **Same currency, different booking rates.** MUST succeed, storing a non-zero signed `RealizedFxDifference`, and MUST post nothing and change no GL figure (§2.9).

## 4. Error Rules

All errors are RFC-7807 ProblemDetails per SDD-INFRA-001 (`title` = code, `detail` = developer English, `type` = `https://finance.local/errors/{code}`). `BaseApiController.ToActionResult` maps codes to HTTP via `IErrorCodeToStatusMap` (SDD-INFRA-009); services return `Result.Failure(code, detail)`. Constants live in `Finance.Common/ErrorCodes/PaymentErrorCodes.cs` (SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE) — the file SDD-PAY-001 creates, which this spec ADDS to rather than duplicating. `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION` is referenced from `CommonErrorCodes` and `PAGE_SIZE_TOO_LARGE` from `FilterErrorCodes` (SDD-INFRA-005) — NOT redefined.

| Code | HTTP | Trigger | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PAYMENT_NOT_FOUND` | 404 | Payment id does not exist (defined by SDD-PAY-001, referenced here) | Not found |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_NOT_FOUND` | 404 | Allocation id does not exist for the route payment | Not found |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` | 404 | The `InvoiceId` is unknown to the local `InvoiceOpenItem` projection — either a transient projection lag, or a document type the §2.3 admission rule never projects (v1: `CreditNote`), which is permanent (§2.3) | Not found (projection lag or non-settleable type) |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_ITEMS_REQUIRED` | 400 | Allocate called with a missing or empty item list | Validation (shape) |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_REQUIRED` | 400 | An item omits `InvoiceId` | Validation (shape) |
| `INVALID_PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_AMOUNT` | 400 | Item amount ≤ 0, or more than 2 decimal places | Validation (shape) |
| `PAGE_SIZE_TOO_LARGE` | 400 | `FilterRequest.PageSize` exceeds 200 (from SDD-INFRA-005) | Validation (paging) |
| `PAYMENT_NOT_ALLOCATABLE` | 409 | Payment status is not `Confirmed` or `Posted` | Conflict (state) |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` | 409 | The invoice open item's status is not `Confirmed` or `Posted` — it is the terminal `Cancelled` (incl. a §2.3 tombstone) or `Reversed` | Conflict (state) |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_PAYMENT` | 409 | Existing plus requested allocations exceed `Payment.Amount` | Conflict (over-allocation) |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_OUTSTANDING` | 409 | An invoice's `SettledAmount` plus requested exceeds its `GrossTotal` | Conflict (over-allocation) |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_DIRECTION_MISMATCH` | 409 | Payment `Direction` differs from the invoice `Direction` | Conflict (matching) |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_COUNTERPARTY_MISMATCH` | 409 | Payment `CounterpartyId` differs from the invoice `CounterpartyId` | Conflict (matching) |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_CURRENCY_MISMATCH` | 409 | Payment `CurrencyCode` differs from the invoice `CurrencyCode` — cross-currency allocation is not supported in v1 | Conflict (matching) |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_CONTROL_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH` | 409 | The payment and invoice document types are not a documented `SettlementPairing` pair, so the two documents moved different control accounts (e.g. a `SupplierPayment` against a customer `CreditNote`). **Defensive code** (§2.5): unreachable through the v1 paths, since §2.3 admits only settleable document types into the projection — retained as defense-in-depth and for a future `SettlementPairing` widening | Conflict (matching, defensive) |
| `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_DUPLICATE` | 409 | A row already exists, or is requested twice, for the `(PaymentId, InvoiceId)` pair | Conflict (uniqueness) |
| `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION` | 409 | Stale or malformed `RowVersion` on the payment or the invoice open item — including a `RowVersion` bumped by ordinary §2.3 projection maintenance | Conflict (concurrency) |

`DefaultErrorCodeToStatusMap` already classifies the three `*_NOT_FOUND` codes as **404**, `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_DUPLICATE` as **409** (the `*DUPLICATE*` pattern), and `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION` as **409** (the `CONCURRENT_*` pattern). The remaining EIGHT conflicts — `PAYMENT_NOT_ALLOCATABLE`, `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_PAYMENT`, `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_OUTSTANDING`, and the four `*_MISMATCH` codes (direction, counterparty, currency, control account) — match no default pattern and MUST be added explicitly to `PaymentErrorCodeToStatusMap` (the domain map SDD-PAY-001 registers via `services.Replace(...)`), or they will fall through to **400**. The validation codes → 400.

**The map is ONE class shared with SDD-PAY-001 — this is the reciprocal of its §4 (MUST).** `PaymentErrorCodeToStatusMap` is created and registered exactly once, by SDD-PAY-001 §4, and it MUST carry the EIGHT codes from THAT spec (`PAYMENT_NOT_DRAFT`, `PAYMENT_NOT_CONFIRMED`, `PAYMENT_POSTING_PENDING`, `PAYMENT_POSTED_IMMUTABLE`, `INVALID_PAYMENT_STATE_TRANSITION`, `PAYMENT_PERIOD_CLOSED`, `PAYMENT_HAS_ALLOCATIONS`, `PAYMENT_DATE_YEAR_MISMATCH`) PLUS the EIGHT above — so once both specs ship in this batch the map MUST carry exactly **SIXTEEN** explicit entries and delegate every OTHER code to a private `new DefaultErrorCodeToStatusMap()`. Built from SDD-PAY-001 §4 alone the map would leave this spec's eight falling through to **400** where this section documents **409**, a client-visible contract break. SDD-PAY-003 adds NOTHING: all six of its codes are 400 validation codes that MUST resolve through the default map. The §6.5 map test pins THIS spec's eight to 409 and asserts that `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_DUPLICATE`, the `*_NOT_FOUND` codes and `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION` resolve through the default map with no explicit entry; SDD-PAY-001 §6.4's test pins its own eight the same way. The two counts MUST agree with each other and with those two tests — 8 + 8 = 16.

**Cross-service note (SDD-INV-001).** `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` is a genuine **404**, not a `503`: from the caller's perspective the invoice is not (yet) an allocatable open item. It MUST NOT be re-mapped to a service-unavailable code merely because the underlying cause may be projection lag, and the frontend copy for it SHOULD suggest retrying (§2.3). The code now carries TWO causes and the copy MUST NOT promise that a retry will help: projection lag clears on its own, whereas a document type the admission rule excludes (v1: `CreditNote`) will NEVER become allocatable in v1, so the copy SHOULD say "not an allocatable open item — if it was just confirmed, retry" rather than "retry" alone. A separate error code for the permanent cause is NOT introduced: the allocation chain deliberately learns nothing about an invoice absent from its projection, and inventing one would require the cross-service read §2.3 forbids.

**Concurrency note.** `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION` on this surface does NOT imply two operators competing. It also arises from ordinary projection maintenance: a §2.3 consumer applying an invoice event mid-allocation bumps `InvoiceOpenItem.RowVersion` and fails an otherwise uncontended allocation (§2.14). It is a benign, retryable outcome, not a data defect, so the frontend copy for it SHOULD invite the operator to retry rather than reporting a conflict with another user.

**Frontend obligation.** Every code above MUST get a matching `errors.<CODE>` entry in BOTH `frontend/src/shared/i18n/locales/en.ts` and `bg.ts` in the SAME PR as the payments frontend (SDD-UI-001). Backend-first this batch; recorded for the frontend phase.

## 5. Versioning Notes

`/api/v1/payments/{id}/allocations/*` is the v1 surface: `GET` (list), `POST` (allocate against an explicit invoice list), `DELETE /{allocationId}` (deallocate).

- **v1 — Initial specification (Phase 5 — Payments).** The `PaymentAllocation` entity (`INT IDENTITY` PK, no-FK cross-service `InvoiceId`); the event-fed `InvoiceOpenItem` local projection with FOUR idempotent consumers, the `SettlementPairing`-driven settleable-document-type admission rule (§2.3) and a stated eventual-consistency contract; allocate/deallocate/list with RBAC; the TEN-rule cross-aggregate invariant chain via `IChainValidator<PaymentAllocationValidationContext>` (SDD-INFRA-007), including the `SettlementPairing` control-account rule; the derived `SettlementStatus` by exact decimal comparison; maintenance of `Payment.AllocatedAmount` and `InvoiceOpenItem.SettledAmount` in the same transaction; audit-first → outbox `PaymentAllocatedEvent` / `PaymentDeallocatedEvent`; the one-way settlement handshake to the Invoices service; the computed-and-stored `RealizedFxDifference` behind a dormant `IRealizedFxHandler`; no caching.
- **`InvoiceConfirmedEvent` growth (additive, non-breaking — SDD-INV-001 §5).** This spec REQUIRES two additive fields on the shipped event, `DueDate` and `BookingExchangeRate`, supplied by the SDD-INV-001 amendment. `BookingExchangeRate` is sourced from a new `Invoice.ExchangeRate` column frozen at invoice creation (SDD-INV-001 §2.14), so the projection receives the REAL rate the invoice froze rather than a placeholder. Adding event fields and adding that column are both additive; the §2.2 fallback is a contingency for a slipped amendment, not a supported deployment mode.
- **`InvoiceReversedEvent` (new event, additive — SDD-INV-001 §2.7).** This spec REQUIRES a new `InvoiceReversedEvent` published by the SDD-INV-001 amendment from its reversal path, and consumes it in the fourth projection consumer (§2.3). Adding a new event type is additive and breaks no existing contract, but the REQUIREMENT is not optional: without it §2.5 rule 3 is unenforceable for reversed invoices, since the projection would keep reading `Posted`.
- **New enum, new map + new events (additive).** `SettlementStatus` (this spec's §2.8, in `Finance.Common` and shared with SDD-INV-001 — there is exactly one settlement enum), the shared `SettlementPairing` table (§2.5 rule 10), and the two payment allocation events are new types; no existing contract changes shape. WIDENING a `SettlementPairing` entry (e.g. admitting `CreditNote`) is additive, and because §2.3's admission predicate is DERIVED from the same table it widens the projection with no second edit — it does, however, require a one-off backfill of the invoices skipped while the type was excluded, and it makes §2.5 rule 10 reachable again. NARROWING an entry is breaking, because a pair that was allocatable stops being so AND the projection stops admitting a type it previously held.
- **Deferred (future versions / specs):**
  - **Posting the realized FX difference** — SDD-FIN-005 will supply the real handler (posting the difference to the country's FX gain/loss accounts through `IPostingEngine`, SDD-FIN-006, using the same event handshake pattern as SDD-PAY-001's posting). The seam is the ONLY extension point: no change to the allocation code, the column, or the events is required, so the change is additive.
  - **Cross-currency allocation** — SDD-FIN-005; relaxing the currency-match invariant changes the invariant set and is therefore breaking (§2.5).
  - **Credit-note settlement** — deferred with the refund / offset feature (a future `CHG-FEAT-*`, alongside SDD-PAY-001 §1's refund document type and `Compensation` method). Until then a `CreditNote` is not admitted into the projection at all (§2.3), so it is neither allocatable nor aged (SDD-PAY-003 §2.1/§5) rather than being an open item nothing can settle. It requires an accountant decision on the `CREDIT_NOTE` control account (SDD-PAY-001 §2.13 / §7 sign-off gate); once decided, admitting the pair is an additive `SettlementPairing` widening plus a backfill of the skipped notes plus a test — no schema change and no consumer change.
  - **A GL-level realized-FX reconciliation** — blocked on SDD-FIN-005 for a second reason beyond the missing handler: the ledger currently posts transactional amounts into its base-amount columns for every document type (§2.9), so there is no rate-converted GL figure to reconcile against. Fixing that is SDD-FIN-005's, not this spec's.
  - **FIFO / oldest-due-first auto-matching** — a future `CHG-FEAT-*` (additive: an optional strategy field or a `POST .../allocations/auto` sub-resource; the explicit-list contract stays valid).
  - **Settlement tolerance / residual write-off** — a future `CHG-ENH-*`; changing the exact-comparison derivation (§2.8) is breaking and requires `/api/v2/`.
  - **In-place amendment of an allocation amount** — a future additive `PUT .../allocations/{allocationId}`; v1 requires deallocate + re-allocate.
  - **Nightly projection reconciliation** — §7; operational, no contract change.
  - **AP/AR aging & counterparty balances** over this projection — SDD-PAY-003 (read-only; no change here).
- Adding an event field, a DTO field, or a filterable property is additive; changing the settlement derivation (adding tolerance), the invariant set (permitting over-allocation or cross-currency matching), or the `(PaymentId, InvoiceId)` uniqueness rule is breaking and requires `/api/v2/` plus a `CHG-ENH-*` (+ a migration for the unique index).

## 7. Resolved Decisions & Open Items

### Resolved
- **Allocation is sub-ledger matching, not posting.** No journal entry is created, mutated, or reversed; no GL or trial-balance figure changes (SDD-FIN-003); `IWorkflowEngine<Payment>` is not invoked. Consequently allocation rows are mutable/removable — corrections are deletes, not sign-flipped reversals.
- **`PaymentAllocation` shape.** `INT IDENTITY` PK (internal child, never individually event-exposed), `UNIQUEIDENTIFIER` `PaymentId` FK with cascade, `UNIQUEIDENTIFIER` `InvoiceId` with NO FK, `DECIMAL(18,2)` amounts / `DECIMAL(18,6)` rates, `DATETIMEOFFSET` timestamps, `correlation_id` column, `rowversion`, schema `payments`, unique index on `(PaymentId, InvoiceId)`.
- **A local event-fed projection, not a synchronous read.** `InvoiceOpenItem` (PK = the mirrored `InvoiceId`) is fed by FOUR invoice events through idempotent consumers — the three shipped ones plus the new `InvoiceReversedEvent` — so allocation and aging never cross-join `finance_invoices` and never depend on the Invoices service being up. Eventual consistency is stated in the spec and surfaced as a 404, not hidden.
- **`Cancelled` and `Reversed` are terminal in the projection, and an unknown cancellation tombstones.** A cancellation for an `InvoiceId` the projection has not seen upserts a `Cancelled` row with default financial values rather than no-opping, and no consumer may move a row out of `Cancelled`/`Reversed` (§2.3). Both rules exist to close the same hole: a projection that can be talked back into `Confirmed`/`Posted` makes a void document a legal allocation target for real cash.
- **Only SETTLEABLE document types are projected.** The `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` consumer skips — silently and successfully, no row and no throw — any invoice whose `DocumentType` no `SettlementPairing` entry can settle, which in v1 is `CreditNote` alone (§2.3). The predicate is `SettlementPairing.IsSettleableInvoiceType`, DERIVED from the same pairs as the §2.5 rule-10 allocation rule, so admission and allocation cannot drift and a `SettlementPairing` widening widens both. Without the skip a credit note would be an open item nothing could ever settle: `DirectionFor(CreditNote) = AP` while the note moves the CUSTOMER control account, so it would age as a phantom AP payable through every bucket forever while the AR sub-ledger read `0.00` against a non-zero GL control account. The residual cost is stated rather than hidden — `InvoicePostedEvent`/`InvoiceReversedEvent` carry no `DocumentType`, so a posted credit note's back-event dead-letters as expected noise, never as drift (open item below).
- **Matching is validated on the CONTROL ACCOUNT, not on `Direction`.** `InvoiceDirection` encodes CASH direction and groups `SaleInvoice` with `DebitNote` (`AR`) and `PurchaseInvoice` with `CreditNote` (`AP`), so equal directions do not imply equal control accounts. §2.5 rule 10 therefore pairs document types through one shared `SettlementPairing` table (`CustomerReceipt → { SaleInvoice, DebitNote }`, `SupplierPayment → { PurchaseInvoice }`) and rejects everything else with `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_CONTROL_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH`. Document-type pairs were chosen over a `ControlAccountFor(...)` account-code map because (a) no `CREDIT_NOTE`/`DEBIT_NOTE` posting rule exists in shipped code — `BulgariaStrategy.BuildDefaultRules()` returns exactly `SALE_INVOICE`, `PURCHASE_INVOICE`, `CUSTOMER_PAYMENT` — so a credit note's control account is undetermined pending the SDD-PAY-001 §7 accountant sign-off, and (b) account codes are country data (`411`/`401`/`503` are Bulgarian NCA codes) that SDD-CTRY-001 keeps behind `ICountryStrategy`, out of `Finance.Common`. The direction rule is retained as the cheaper pre-filter. If the `DEBIT_NOTE` template's control account changes at sign-off, `SettlementPairing` is the single place to revisit. Rule 10 itself is UNREACHABLE through the v1 paths once the admission rule above keeps `CreditNote` out of the projection — every remaining mismatch is caught by the direction rule or by rule 2 — so it, its error code and its edge case are retained as defense-in-depth and tested against a directly-seeded open item; a `SettlementPairing` widening makes it live again (§2.5, §5).
- **`SettledAmount` is locally owned.** It is maintained from this database's allocation rows in the same transaction; the consumers never write it, and the Invoices service keeps its own independent copy from the events published here.
- **Invariants as an `IChainValidator` chain.** All TEN rules are cross-aggregate/stateful, so they live in `Validation/` as one validator per rule registered in a documented order (SDD-INFRA-007); only shape rules stay in FluentValidation.
- **Over-allocation forbidden; `SettlementStatus` derived by exact comparison.** No tolerance band, no write-off, no unapplied-cash suspense rule in v1. `SettlementStatus` is a derived enum computed in one calculator, never a stored workflow state.
- **One-way handshake to Invoices, no back-event.** Payments publishes one event per allocation row through its outbox and is done; the invoice-side update belongs to the SDD-INV-001 amendment. This differs deliberately from SDD-PAY-001's posting handshake, which needs a dedicated back-event to receive the `JournalEntryId`.
- **Explicit invoice list in v1.** FIFO / oldest-due-first auto-matching is deferred; an empty item list is a validation error, never an implicit "apply everything".
- **Realized FX computed and stored, posting deferred behind a dormant seam.** `RealizedFxDifference` is stored signed and handed to `IRealizedFxHandler`, whose only registered implementation is `NoOpRealizedFxHandler`, modelled on SDD-FIN-002 §2.7's `IPostingPeriodGuard` + always-open default. Informational only until SDD-FIN-005 lands. It is a DOCUMENT-level difference derived from the two aggregates' frozen rates — NOT a reconciliation of GL base balances: the shipped `PostingEngine.BuildLine` pins every line to `ExchangeRate = 1.000000` and writes the transactional amount into the base-amount columns for every document type, a pre-existing SDD-FIN-006 limitation this spec neither depends on nor repairs (§2.9).
- **No caching, and permissions.** Allocations/open items/settlement state are transactional data (SDD-INFRA-004). `finance.payment:read` for the list; the new `finance.payment:allocate` for both writes, seeded manually in the auth-service while SDD-INT-AUTH-001 §2.4 auto-registration is deferred.
- **Release-on-reverse: allocations are NEVER auto-released.** SDD-PAY-001 §2.7 (with §2.6 and §3.2) already decides this with a MUST — a payment with `AllocatedAmount > 0` is hard-blocked from BOTH cancel and reverse with `PAYMENT_HAS_ALLOCATIONS`, and the operator MUST deallocate first. Nothing in this spec releases a match implicitly. The §2.6 "a `Cancelled`/`Reversed` payment's rows MUST be left intact" clause is therefore defensive code, unreachable through the v1 paths.
- **Exactly one settlement enum.** `SettlementStatus` lives in `src/Finance.Common/Enums/SettlementStatus.cs`, is declared and owned by §2.8, and is shared verbatim by the SDD-INV-001 amendment (`Invoice.SettlementStatus`) and by SDD-PAY-003. No `InvoiceSettlementStatus` fork exists in any spec or in code.

### Open / deferred (for the Phase-2 implementator)
- **`InvoiceConfirmedEvent` additive fields.** `DueDate` and `BookingExchangeRate` MUST be added by the SDD-INV-001 amendment in this batch (additive, non-breaking), with `BookingExchangeRate` sourced from the new `Invoice.ExchangeRate` column that amendment adds and freezes at invoice creation (SDD-INV-001 §2.14) — the projection needs the REAL frozen rate, not a constant. If that slips, the implementator MUST ship the §2.2 contingency fallback (`DueDate := IssueDate`, `BookingExchangeRate := 1.000000`), MUST record it in the change spec, and MUST flag the impact to SDD-PAY-003. Recommendation: ship the amendment — the fallback silently degrades due dates and misstates `RealizedFxDifference` and `BaseOutstanding` for every non-base-currency invoice.
- **`InvoiceReversedEvent` is a hard cross-spec dependency, not a nice-to-have.** The SDD-INV-001 amendment MUST publish it from the §2.7 reversal path in THIS batch; the §2.3 fourth consumer and the §2.5 rule 3 exclusion of `Reversed` are useless without it, and the gap it closes is a real-cash defect (a receipt matched to a voided invoice while the genuinely open one stays outstanding), not a mis-aging one. There is NO fallback: unlike the two additive `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` fields, no local approximation can tell the projection an invoice was reversed. If the amendment slips, the implementator MUST raise it as a blocker rather than shipping allocation against a projection that cannot see reversal.
- **Audit granularity.** §2.11 requires one audit row per allocation row. A single batch row per request is the alternative if per-row audit is judged noisy for large multi-item allocations (mirrors the SDD-FIN-004 generation-audit question). Recommend per-row for completeness; decide once and document.
- **`DocumentType` on `InvoicePostedEvent` / `InvoiceReversedEvent`.** Neither event carries it, so the two consumers cannot recognise an invoice the §2.3 admission rule deliberately skipped, and a posted (or later reversed) `CreditNote` therefore exhausts the retry schedule and dead-letters. That is expected, lossless noise — but it IS noise. Adding `DocumentType` to both events (additive, SDD-INV-001 §5) would let the consumers no-op instead. It is an SDD-INV-001 payload change this batch does not own; recommended to be closed together with credit-note settlement, when the `SettlementPairing` widening removes the skip and the noise with it. Until then operator triage and the reconciliation job below MUST NOT treat those dead letters as drift.
- **Projection reconciliation job.** A nightly job that repairs drifted or DLQ'd open items MAY be added. It MUST be idempotent, MUST NOT write `SettledAmount`, MUST NOT create an open item for a document type the §2.3 admission rule excludes (it MUST read the same `SettlementPairing.IsSettleableInvoiceType` predicate, never its own list), and MUST NOT sit on the request path. Its in-scope repair classes are: an open item missing after a DLQ'd `InvoicePostedEvent` for a SETTLEABLE type; a stale externally-owned column; and the **orphaned settlement** class of §2.3 — allocation rows still pointing at an invoice that reached `Cancelled` because the SDD-INV-001 `INVOICE_HAS_SETTLEMENTS` guard raced an in-flight allocation. Deferred; recommended once the DLQ or the orphan warning shows real drift.
- **Open-item DTO ownership.** This spec declares NO projection-mirror DTO: it has no endpoint that returns the projection (its list endpoint returns `PaymentAllocationDto`, enriched with a few joined invoice fields, §2.7). The read shape for the projection is `OpenItemDto` in `src/Finance.ServiceModel/Payments/OpenItemDto.cs`, owned by SDD-PAY-003 because that spec adds the computed report fields (`Outstanding`, `BaseOutstanding`, `DaysPastDue`, `AgingBucket`). There MUST be exactly one such DTO and this spec MUST NOT declare a second.
- **GUID-keyed loader.** `BaseEntityService.FindOrNotFoundAsync` is `int`-keyed while the payment PK is `UNIQUEIDENTIFIER`. Either grow SDD-INFRA-009 with a `Guid` overload or hand-roll the loader as `InvoiceService.LoadWithLinesAsync` does; decide once with the SDD-PAY-001 implementator, not twice.
- **Per-service infrastructure duplication.** `ICurrentUserAccessor` already exists as five independent per-service copies (Accounts, Invoices, Journal, Nomenclature, Periods), so the Payments copy this spec relies on (§2.1/§2.11) would be the **sixth** — not the third. The same pattern holds for the pieces SDD-PAY-001 needs: `IAccountReadClient` and `IPeriodReadClient` live in `namespace Finance.Journal.API.Interfaces` (`src/Interfaces/Journal/Finance.Journal.API/Interfaces/`) and `BearerTokenForwardingHandler` exists twice (`Finance.Journal.API/Http/` and `Finance.Nomenclature.API/Http/`). None of them are shared contracts. `Finance.Payments.API` MUST NOT project-reference another service's API to reuse them; it ships its own copies under `Finance.Payments.API/Interfaces/` and `Finance.Payments.API/Http/`. Recommendation (shared with SDD-PAY-001): promote `ICurrentUserAccessor`, the two Refit read contracts, and the handler into `Finance.Infrastructure.Web` as a spec-owned change to SDD-INFRA-009, and stop copying them per service.
- **`audit` schema hardening.** The DENY UPDATE/DELETE grants SDD-AUDIT-001 §2.5 requires are still absent from every migration in the repo. Payments inherits the gap; it MUST NOT be papered over in application code.
