## 1. Context & Scope

This spec defines the **`Payment` aggregate** and its **lifecycle**: the state machine that moves a payment through `Draft → Confirmed → Posted` (plus `Cancelled` and the `Reversed` correction path), the side effects each transition triggers, the amount/FX computation that produces its base-currency value, and the handshake by which a confirmed payment becomes a posted journal entry. It is the cash tier of the platform and the third document aggregate after the journal entry (SDD-FIN-002) and the invoice (SDD-INV-001).

A single aggregate `Payment` represents both cash movements — a **Customer Receipt** (money in, AR) and a **Supplier Payment** (money out, AP) — discriminated by a `DocumentType` enum, with a `Direction` (`AR` receivable / `AP` payable) derived from it and frozen, and a `Method` (`Cash` | `BankTransfer` | `Card`) recording how the money moved. The payment records the movement of cash against a **settlement account** (the cash/bank GL account from the Chart of Accounts, SDD-ACCT-001); it does NOT record which invoices it pays. Matching a payment to invoices is sub-ledger allocation and belongs entirely to SDD-PAY-002; aging and counterparty balances belong to SDD-PAY-003.

Three principles govern the lifecycle, mirroring SDD-INV-001:
1. **Confirmed/Posted is immutable (SDD-AUDIT-001).** Once a payment is `Confirmed` it carries a legally-issued gapless document number and MUST NEVER have its header edited. A `Draft` MAY be updated, deleted, or `Cancelled` (voided) — **cancellation is legal from `Draft` ONLY**, because once a payment is `Confirmed` its posting is irrevocably in flight (§2.1, §2.6). A `Posted` payment is corrected ONLY by reversal — a sign-flipped journal entry produced by the Journal service (SDD-FIN-002 §2.6) while the payment row itself is flagged `Reversed`. Nothing is ever UPDATEd out of band and nothing is ever hard-deleted after confirm.
2. **Numbering is gapless and country-formatted.** The gapless counter comes from `ISequenceGenerator.NextValueAsync` (SDD-INFRA-003) at **Confirm** only, and the FORMAT comes from `ICountryStrategy.GenerateDocumentNumber` (SDD-CTRY-001) per document type (`RCT` for a receipt, `PAY` for a supplier payment). Gaplessness is a НАП requirement; numbers are never hand-rolled, never recycled, and never reassigned.
3. **Posting is delegated, never reimplemented.** The Payments service does NOT build double-entry lines, number journal entries, or write GL. On confirm it publishes `PaymentConfirmedEvent` through the transactional outbox; the **Journal** service consumes it and posts the entry via the Posting Engine (SDD-FIN-006) using a seeded posting rule, then publishes back the dedicated `PaymentPostedEvent`. The payment moves to `Posted` and stores the resulting `JournalEntryId` only when that back-event arrives (§2.5). A dedicated back-event is used rather than the generic `JournalEntryPostedEvent` (which is published for every entry and is already consumed by the EventLog archive), exactly as SDD-INV-001 §2.5 resolved it.

**Settlement account, not a bank master file.** `SettlementAccountId` is an `INT` reference to a Chart-of-Accounts account owned by `Finance.Accounts.API`. It MUST be validated as *existing AND active* through the accounts read seam (Refit through the gateway, never a cross-database join, Plan §8). The stricter "is postable / non-header leaf" assertion is a DORMANT seam: the Chart of Accounts exposes no `IsPostable`/`IsHeader` flag today, so this spec asserts existence + `IsActive` and turns the stricter check on when CHG-ENH-002 lands — mirroring the way SDD-FIN-002 modelled its dormant period-lock guard.

**Currency and FX.** A payment MAY be recorded in any active currency. `BaseCurrencyCode` is frozen at creation from `ICountryStrategy.BaseCurrencyCode`; `ExchangeRate` is the rate at `PaymentDate` and MUST be exactly `1.000000` when the payment currency IS the base currency; `BaseAmount = round(Amount × ExchangeRate)` through `ICountryStrategy`. `BaseAmount` and `ExchangeRate` are stored on the payment row for REPORTING and for SDD-PAY-002's realized-FX computation — they are NOT what is posted to the GL in v1: the Journal side posts the TRANSACTIONAL `Amount` in `CurrencyCode`, exactly as the shipped invoice consumer does, so both legs of a control account are booked in the same currency (§2.5, and the known platform limitation recorded in §7). The **realized FX difference** that arises when a payment settles an invoice booked at a different rate is NOT this spec's concern — it is computed and stored per allocation row by SDD-PAY-002 and its GL posting waits on SDD-FIN-005.

**ISA-95 classification.** A `Payment` is an ISA-95 **Level 4 (Business Planning & Logistics)** financial **Document** recording a cash settlement transaction (ISA-95 / IEC 62264 Part 1, §5). The **confirm**, **post**, **cancel**, and **reverse** operations are Level-4 business transactions that change the document's recorded state; each MUST emit an **immutable domain event** (`PaymentConfirmedEvent`, `PaymentCancelledEvent`, `PaymentReversedEvent`; the posted state is reflected by the dedicated `PaymentPostedEvent` published by the Journal service) and an immutable audit row in the same transaction (SDD-AUDIT-001). **Cancel** is legal from `Draft` only (§2.6) but is still a recorded state change with its own event and terminal state — unlike a draft delete, which removes the row. The `PaymentStatusHistory` rows are append-only Level-4 audit sub-records. **Draft create/update/delete are Level-4 operations on a pre-transactional working document:** a `Draft` carries no legally-issued number, so they are NOT business transactions, emit NO domain event, and are recorded only by the immutable audit row (§2.15). The settlement account is Level-4 master data (SDD-ACCT-001) referenced by surrogate id; the counterparty (customer/supplier) is Warehouse-owned Level-4 master data referenced by GUID, NOT a foreign key (cross-service joins are forbidden, Plan §8). No Level-3 (MES) production activity is modelled.

## 2. Behavior

> **Service/controller contract (SDD-INFRA-009).** `PaymentService` MUST inherit `SearchableServiceBase<Payment, PaymentDto, PaymentsDbContext>` (and therefore `BaseEntityService<PaymentsDbContext>`). Every public method MUST return `Result` / `Result<T>` — never `null`, never a thrown exception for a business outcome. Because `BaseEntityService.FindOrNotFoundAsync` is `int`-keyed and `Payment.Id` is a `UNIQUEIDENTIFIER`, the service MUST hand-roll its `Guid` loader (`LoadAsync`) exactly as `InvoiceService.LoadWithLinesAsync` does. `PaymentsController` inherits `BaseApiController` and translates every result via `ToActionResult(...)`. State transitions MUST go through `IWorkflowEngine<Payment>` (SDD-INFRA-008); the service owns `SaveChanges` / `RowVersion` / status-history inside the outbox transaction. `CancellationToken` MUST be threaded controller → service → DB / sequence / period guard / account reader / publish.

### 2.1 State machine (MUST — SDD-INFRA-008)
- `Payment` MUST be a workflow aggregate with these states and `AllowedNextStates`, one `IWorkflowState<Payment>` per state in `src/Interfaces/Payments/Finance.Payments.API/Workflow/`:
  - `Draft` → { `Confirmed`, `Cancelled` } (and a `Draft` MAY be **updated/deleted** — an edit/removal, not a transition).
  - `Confirmed` → { `Posted` } — `Cancelled` is DELIBERATELY absent (see the rationale below).
  - `Posted` → { `Reversed` } (a posted payment is corrected only by reversal, §2.7).
  - `Cancelled` → { } (terminal).
  - `Reversed` → { } (terminal).
- **Cancel is legal from `Draft` ONLY — the accounting rationale (MUST NOT be relaxed without a `CHG-ENH-*`).** By the time a payment is `Confirmed`, `PaymentConfirmedEvent` is already in flight through the outbox and the Journal-side consumer posts REGARDLESS of the payment's later state — its only guard is the §2.5 duplicate-post check for `("Payment", PaymentId)`, which never consults the payment row. A `Confirmed → Cancelled` transition would therefore be able to leave a posted `Dr 503 / Cr 411` entry in the general ledger with no supporting document, and NO in-service correction path would exist: `Cancelled` is terminal (`AllowedNextStates = {}`), the `PaymentPostedEvent` back-event would dead-letter (§2.18), `Reversed` is reachable ONLY from `Posted`, and `PUT`/`DELETE` return `PAYMENT_POSTED_IMMUTABLE`. A document whose entry may already be in the ledger is corrected by **REVERSAL, never by cancellation** — the same principle SDD-AUDIT-001 applies to posted journal entries. The residual operational case (a payment stuck in `Confirmed` because the Journal never posted) is handled by re-driving `POST /{id}/post`, which RE-ENQUEUES `PaymentConfirmedEvent` (§2.5) — not by cancelling (§2.18).
- Any transition not in `AllowedNextStates` — including `Confirmed → Cancelled` — MUST be rejected by the engine and surfaced as `INVALID_PAYMENT_STATE_TRANSITION` (the domain alias for the engine's generic `INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`, SDD-INFRA-008 §4, mirroring SDD-FIN-002 §2.1 and SDD-INV-001 §2.1).
- `Status` MUST be persisted as a string (`HasConversion<string>()`) so the workflow engine resolves states by name; `StateName` MUST be `nameof(PaymentStatus.X)`.
- All state side effects MUST live in `PaymentService` — `OnEnterAsync`/`OnExitAsync` MUST return `Task.CompletedTask` (mirroring the shipped invoice states).
- A new payment MUST be created in `Draft` (§2.3).

### 2.2 Workflow guards & ordering (MUST — SDD-INFRA-007)
- The `Draft → Confirmed` transition MUST run, in order:
  1. The full validation surface (§3): document type, method, counterparty, currency, `Amount > 0`, exchange rate, payment date, settlement account.
  2. The **settlement-account guard** — the account MUST exist (`PAYMENT_SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`) and be active (`PAYMENT_SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNT_INACTIVE`) per §2.8.
  3. The **period-open guard** for `PaymentDate` — a `Closed` period, a date with no period, or an unreachable Periods service MUST short-circuit with `PAYMENT_PERIOD_CLOSED` (§2.9).
  4. The **document-number year guard** — `PaymentDate.Year` MUST equal the confirm-clock year; a mismatch MUST short-circuit with `PAYMENT_DATE_YEAR_MISMATCH` before any sequence value is consumed (§2.4).
- `PaymentPeriodWorkflowGuard` MUST be an `IChainValidator<WorkflowContext<Payment>>` that is INERT unless `TargetState == nameof(PaymentStatus.Confirmed)`, mirroring the shipped `InvoicePeriodWorkflowGuard`.
- A guard failure MUST short-circuit with no side effects — no sequence value consumed, no event enqueued, no audit row, no status-history row — and surface as `Result.Failure(...)` carrying the failing guard's code (translated from the engine's `WORKFLOW_GUARD_FAILED` + `Detail`, SDD-INFRA-008 §4).

### 2.3 Create draft (MUST)
- `POST /api/v1/payments` MUST create a `Payment` in `Draft` from `CreatePaymentRequest` (document type, method, counterparty, currency, amount, exchange rate, settlement account, payment date, optional bank reference). Requires `finance.payment:create`. Returns 201 (`CreatedAtAction`), 400, 404, 409.
- `Direction` MUST be DERIVED from `DocumentType` and FROZEN: `CustomerReceipt` → `AR`; `SupplierPayment` → `AP`. A client-supplied direction MUST be ignored. The derivation MUST live in `PaymentDocumentTypeMap.DirectionFor` alongside `SequenceKeyFor` and `PostingRuleKeyFor` (mirroring `InvoiceDocumentTypeMap`).
- **An unrecognized `DocumentType` MUST be a business FAILURE, not an exception — `PaymentDocumentTypeMap` MUST expose `IsSupported(PaymentDocumentType)` and `CreateDraftAsync` MUST call it BEFORE it builds the draft.** All three per-type maps (`SequenceKeyFor`, `DirectionFor`, `PostingRuleKeyFor`) `throw new ArgumentOutOfRangeException` on an unknown value, and SDD-INFRA-009 forbids throwing for a business outcome, so the service MUST return `Result<PaymentDto>.Failure(INVALID_PAYMENT_DOCUMENT_TYPE)` and persist nothing. The HTTP path is already screened by `CreatePaymentRequestValidator` (§3.1); this guard covers the non-HTTP callers this section anticipates (see the last bullet). Shipped as `PaymentDocumentTypeMap.IsSupported` (`src/Interfaces/Payments/Finance.Payments.API/Services/PaymentDocumentTypeMap.cs:57-58`), called at `PaymentService.CreateDraftAsync` (`PaymentService.cs:124-129`) before `BuildDraft`, and pinned by three `[Unit]` tests (§6.4).
- `DocumentNumber` MUST remain NULL while `Draft` — the gapless number is assigned only at Confirm (§2.4).
- `BaseCurrencyCode` MUST be set from `ICountryStrategy.BaseCurrencyCode` and frozen; `BaseAmount` MUST be computed per §2.8 and stored; `AllocatedAmount` MUST be `0.00`; `JournalEntryId`, `CancellationReason`, `ConfirmedAt`, `ConfirmedBy`, `PostedAt`, `ReversedAt` MUST be NULL.
- `CorrelationId` MUST be captured from `ICorrelationIdAccessor`; `CreatedAt` server-side (`SYSDATETIMEOFFSET()`); `CreatedBy` from the authenticated principal via `ICurrentUserAccessor`.
- Draft creation MUST write an audit `Create` row (`BeforeJson = null`, `AuditOperation.Create`). No domain event is published on draft creation.
- Unlike SDD-INV-001 there is NO system/event-driven create path in v1 — every payment is operator-entered. A future Warehouse- or bank-triggered create MUST reuse this same service method, never a parallel path.

### 2.4 Confirm (MUST)
- `POST /api/v1/payments/{id}/confirm` MUST transition a `Draft` payment to `Confirmed` via `IWorkflowEngine<Payment>`. Requires `finance.payment:confirm`. Returns 200, 400, 404, 409.
- The payment MUST be in `Draft`; otherwise `PAYMENT_NOT_DRAFT`. A draft that already carries a `DocumentNumber` MUST be rejected with `PAYMENT_DUPLICATE_DOCUMENT_NUMBER` (replay protection — never a second gapless number).
- Confirm MUST run in two phases: the §2.2 guards BEFORE the transaction opens (steps 1–2), then the state change inside a single SaveChanges/outbox transaction (steps 3–8), in this order:
  1. Recompute and freeze `BaseAmount` via `ICountryStrategy` (§2.8); re-assert the base-amount invariant (`PAYMENT_BASE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH` if it fails).
  2. Run the §2.2 guards (validation + settlement account + period-open + document-number year). These MUST run before the transaction opens, so a guard failure consumes no sequence value, writes no audit row, and enqueues no outbox message (§2.2, §2.9) — a burnt gapless number can never be recycled.
  3. Open the transaction, take the `BeforeJson` draft snapshot, and transition `Draft → Confirmed` through `IWorkflowEngine<Payment>` (§2.1) — the engine is what sets `Status`, so an illegal transition or a failed chain guard aborts here with nothing else done.
  4. Assign `DocumentNumber`: `ISequenceGenerator.NextValueAsync(<key>, ct)` where `<key>` is `SequenceKeys.Receipt` (`RCT`) for a `CustomerReceipt` and `SequenceKeys.Payment` (`PAY`) for a `SupplierPayment` — both keys and their `SequenceDefinition` entries ALREADY exist with `Prefix = "RCT"` / `Prefix = "PAY"`, padding 6, yearly reset (SDD-INFRA-003 §2.1, already `Implemented`) and MUST NOT be redefined, renamed, or re-prefixed by this spec — then format the raw counter via `ICountryStrategy.GenerateDocumentNumber` (§5), which MUST produce exactly `RCT-{yyyy}-{nnnnnn}` for a `CustomerReceipt` and `PAY-{yyyy}-{nnnnnn}` for a `SupplierPayment`, matching the format SDD-INFRA-003 §2.1 already documents. The value MUST be allocated INSIDE the same transaction so a rollback burns no number (SDD-INFRA-003 §2.4, `CHG-FIX-001`).
     - **`{yyyy}` is the CONFIRM-clock year, not `PaymentDate.Year`.** The shipped `BulgariaStrategy.GenerateDocumentNumber` takes only `(documentType, sequenceValue)` and derives the year from the ambient clock (`DateTimeOffset.UtcNow.Year`), and the shipped `SequenceGenerator` composes its yearly reset key from `_timeProvider.GetUtcNow()` (`SequenceKeyComposer.PeriodSegment`, `{key}:{yyyy}`) — so the number's year and the counter series are BOTH pinned to the confirm clock. The new payment-typed overload MUST keep that signature (§5): passing a date into `GenerateDocumentNumber` would require a coordinated SDD-INFRA-003 change this batch does not own.
     - Consequently the §2.2 step-4 **document-number year guard** MUST reject a confirm whose `PaymentDate.Year` differs from the confirm-clock year with `PAYMENT_DATE_YEAR_MISMATCH` (409). Without it a December-2026 receipt confirmed on 2027-01-03 would draw `RCT-2027-000001` while its journal entry posts into the 2026 period — the 2026 series permanently short a number and a 2027-numbered document sitting in the 2026 books, uncorrectable under НАП. The operational consequence is recorded in §7.
  5. Stamp `ConfirmedAt = SYSDATETIMEOFFSET()` and `ConfirmedBy` (re-affirming `Status = Confirmed`).
  6. Append the `PaymentStatusHistory` row (`Draft → Confirmed`) and increment `RowVersion`.
  7. Write an audit `StateChange` row (`EventType = "PaymentConfirmed"`, `BeforeJson` = draft snapshot, `AfterJson` = confirmed snapshot) **before** the outbox row (audit-first, SDD-AUDIT-001).
  8. Enqueue `PaymentConfirmedEvent` to the outbox (atomic with the DB write — no `await _bus.Publish` outside the outbox, no try/catch, SDD-INFRA-006).
  - **Only two orderings in this list are load-bearing, and both are tested.** The guards before any sequence value is drawn (steps 1–2 before step 4) and the audit row before the outbox row (step 7 before step 8, `Confirm_RecordsAuditStateChange_BeforeOutboxPublish`, §6.2). Everything between them commits or rolls back as one unit, so the position of the `PaymentStatusHistory` append relative to the audit row and the publish is NOT observable to any reader. This list is written in the shipped order — `PaymentService.ConfirmInTransactionAsync` appends the history row at `PaymentService.cs:624`, records the audit row at `:626`, and publishes at `:639` — so the spec and the code read the same way. The §2.7 reverse enumeration already matches its own implementation step-for-step (`ReverseInTransactionAsync`: transition → `ReversedAt` → history → audit → publish) and is unchanged.
- Confirm is NOT on SDD-AUDIT-001's mandatory-`Reason` list (it is a routine issuance), so no `Reason` is required. `ConfirmPaymentRequest` carries only `RowVersion` and therefore needs no FluentValidation validator (mirroring the shipped invoice confirm/post requests).

### 2.5 Posting handshake — Confirm → Posted (MUST)
- **Decision (chosen): event-driven with a dedicated back-event**, identical in shape to SDD-INV-001 §2.5. Confirm publishes `PaymentConfirmedEvent`; the **Journal** service is the sole authority that posts the entry; it publishes back the dedicated `PaymentPostedEvent`; the **Payments** service consumes that event, stores `JournalEntryId`, and moves `Confirmed → Posted`. The Payments service MUST NEVER call `IPostingEngine.ApplyAsync` itself (the engine reads `journal.PostingRules` from `JournalDbContext` — an in-process call is impossible across the database-per-service boundary, Plan §8).
- `PaymentConfirmedEvent` MUST carry enough for the Journal side to materialize a balanced entry with no callback: `PaymentId`, `DocumentNumber`, `DocumentType`, `Direction`, `Method`, `CounterpartyId`, `SettlementAccountId`, `CurrencyCode`, `BaseCurrencyCode`, `Amount`, `ExchangeRate`, `BaseAmount`, `PaymentDate`, `PostingRuleKey` (from `PaymentDocumentTypeMap.PostingRuleKeyFor`), plus `MessageId`/`CorrelationId`/`OccurredAt`.
- The Journal-side consumer MUST be `src/Interfaces/Journal/Finance.Journal.API/Consumers/PaymentConfirmedEventConsumer.cs`, registered in that service's `ConfigureConsumers` and automatically wrapped by `IdempotencyFilter<T>`. It MUST build `ApplyPostingRuleRequest { RuleKey = message.PostingRuleKey, Amounts = { [PostingAmountSource.Gross] = message.Amount }, CurrencyCode = message.CurrencyCode, EntryDate = message.PaymentDate, Description = $"Payment {message.DocumentNumber}", PostImmediately = true, SourceDocumentType = JournalSourceDocumentTypes.Payment, SourceDocumentId = message.PaymentId }` and call `IPostingEngine.ApplyAsync` — but only after the duplicate-post check below.
- The amount context MUST use the EXISTING `PostingAmountSource.Gross` only — v1 MUST NOT grow the enum.
- **Duplicate-post backstop — the source-document link on `JournalEntry` (MUST; additive to SDD-FIN-002).** Transport-level dedupe alone cannot prevent a duplicate journal entry: `IdempotencyFilter<T>`'s Redis `SETNX` claim expires after 7 days, and a `finance-journal_error` DLQ replay is an explicit operator action, so a redelivered `PaymentConfirmedEvent` outside that window would post a SECOND entry — cash `503` overstated and `411` understated by the payment amount, with the trial balance still balancing, the sub-ledger still reporting the invoice settled once, and `IX_Payments_JournalEntryId UNIQUE` blocking only a second LINK, never a second POST. The shipped `InvoiceConfirmedEventConsumer` has the identical exposure. Therefore, mirroring what `Invoice` already does for its Warehouse origins (`src/Databases/Finance.Invoices.DBModel/Models/Invoice.cs`, `Guid? SourceDocumentId` + `string? SourceDocumentType`, unique filtered index `IX_Invoices_SourceDocument`):
  - `JournalEntry` (`src/Databases/Finance.Journal.DBModel/Models/JournalEntry.cs`, which today carries only `ReversesEntryId` and no source-document reference) MUST gain two ADDITIVE nullable columns — `SourceDocumentType` (`string?`, `NVARCHAR(40) NULL`) and `SourceDocumentId` (`Guid?`, `UNIQUEIDENTIFIER NULL`) — configured explicitly in `JournalEntryConfiguration` (Fluent API only) and delivered by a **NEW** Journal migration `src/Databases/Finance.Journal.DBModel/Migrations/<timestamp>_AddJournalEntrySourceDocument.cs` with a hand-written XML `<summary>` citing SDD-PAY-001. The two already-applied Journal migrations (`20260603101540_InitialCreate`, `20260604094913_AddPostingRules`) MUST NOT be edited.
  - The **DB backstop** MUST be a UNIQUE FILTERED index `IX_JournalEntries_SourceDocument` over `(SourceDocumentType, SourceDocumentId)` with filter `[SourceDocumentType] IS NOT NULL AND [SourceDocumentId] IS NOT NULL AND [Status] = 'Posted'` — i.e. at most ONE `Posted` entry may ever exist per source document, while drafts, reversed entries, and manually created entries (both columns NULL) are unconstrained. The `Status = 'Posted'` term is expressible because `JournalEntryConfiguration` already stores the status as a string (`HasConversion<string>()`).
  - The source-document tag values MUST be declared as constants (mirroring `Finance.Invoices.API/Consumers/WarehouseSourceDocumentTypes.cs`) in `Finance.Journal.API/Consumers/JournalSourceDocumentTypes.cs`: `Payment = "Payment"` and `Invoice = "Invoice"`.
  - The pair MUST reach the entry through ADDITIVE, OPTIONAL, NULLABLE properties on `ApplyPostingRuleRequest` (SDD-FIN-006) and `CreateJournalEntryRequest` (SDD-FIN-002), copied by `PostingEngine.DelegateToJournalAsync` into the create request and stamped by `JournalEntryService.BuildDraft`. Both records are `sealed record`s whose existing members are `required`; adding two nullable, non-`required` properties leaves every shipped caller (the journal controller, the invoice consumer, the posting endpoint) compiling and behaving unchanged, so the growth is additive (§5).
  - `IJournalEntryService` MUST gain `Task<JournalEntryDto?> FindPostedBySourceDocumentAsync(string sourceDocumentType, Guid sourceDocumentId, CancellationToken cancellationToken)` — the Journal-side mirror of the shipped `IInvoiceService.FindBySourceDocumentAsync` dedupe lookup — returning the `Posted` entry for the pair or `null`.
  - The Journal-side `PaymentConfirmedEventConsumer` MUST call that lookup for `(JournalSourceDocumentTypes.Payment, message.PaymentId)` BEFORE `ApplyAsync` and, when a `Posted` entry already exists, MUST treat the message as a **no-op success** (`Result.Success()`; no `ApplyAsync` call, no second entry, no throw) and MUST stamp `SourceDocumentType = "Payment"`, `SourceDocumentId = message.PaymentId` on every entry it DOES create.
  - **The no-op path MUST still re-publish `PaymentPostedEvent`** for the already-existing entry (its `Id` and `EntryNumber`, `CorrelationId` propagated from the source event, a fresh `MessageId`) through the Journal outbox. Without that, a payment whose entry posted but whose back-event was lost or dead-lettered could never be recovered — which is exactly the case the §2.5 operator post path and §2.18 assign to this mechanism. Re-publishing is safe because the Payments-side consumer is idempotent at the aggregate level (already-`Posted` ⇒ no-op).
  - The **reversing** entry MUST leave both columns NULL. `JournalEntryService.BuildReversal` constructs a fresh `JournalEntry` and copies neither column, so no change is required there — but the omission MUST NOT be "fixed" by copying the pair, or the offsetting entry would claim the source document's index slot.
  - The SHIPPED `InvoiceConfirmedEventConsumer` MUST gain the SAME stamping (`SourceDocumentType = "Invoice"`, `SourceDocumentId = message.InvoiceId`) and the same already-`Posted` no-op (re-publishing `InvoicePostedEvent` for the existing entry). This is an additive hardening of shipped code that ships WITH this batch and is recorded in the SDD-INV-001 amendment's change log.
- **The TRANSACTIONAL `Amount` MUST be posted in `CurrencyCode` — never `BaseAmount` in `BaseCurrencyCode`.** This is not a preference; it is required for the two legs of a control account to net. The shipped `InvoiceConfirmedEventConsumer` (`src/Interfaces/Journal/Finance.Journal.API/Consumers/InvoiceConfirmedEventConsumer.cs`) posts `CurrencyCode = message.CurrencyCode` with the transactional `NetTotal`/`TaxTotal`/`GrossTotal`, so a EUR 1000 sale invoice books `Dr 411 = 1000.00`. If the payment consumer posted the base amount instead, that invoice's full EUR 1000 receipt at rate `1.955830` would book `Cr 411 = 1955.83`, leaving a permanent `955.83` residual on the AR control account while SDD-PAY-002 derives `Settled` and SDD-PAY-003 reports `Outstanding = 0.00`. Posting the transactional amount in the payment's own currency keeps invoice and payment legs symmetrical. The shipped `LineBaseAmountValidator` accepts this (`ExchangeRate = 1.000000` with `Base*Amount == *Amount` reconciles), exactly as it accepts the invoice consumer's output.
- `BaseAmount` and `ExchangeRate` remain STORED on the `Payment` aggregate (§2.8) for reporting and for SDD-PAY-002's realized-FX computation — they are simply NOT what is posted in v1. The known GL currency-naivety this exposes is recorded as a platform limitation in §7; it predates this batch and MUST NOT be "fixed" here.
- On success the Journal service MUST publish `PaymentPostedEvent` through ITS OWN outbox, propagating `CorrelationId` from the source event (never re-reading the ambient one) and carrying `PaymentId`, `JournalEntryId`, `JournalEntryNumber`.
- The inbound consumer `src/Interfaces/Payments/Finance.Payments.API/Consumers/PaymentPostedEventConsumer.cs` MUST call `IPaymentService.LinkPostedJournalEntryAsync(paymentId, journalEntryId, ct)`, which MUST: return `PAYMENT_NOT_FOUND` for an unknown id; return `Result.Success()` as a NO-OP when the payment is already `Posted` (replay-safe); return `PAYMENT_NOT_CONFIRMED` for any other non-`Confirmed` state; otherwise set `JournalEntryId`, stamp `PostedAt`, transition `Confirmed → Posted` through the engine, append status history, write an audit `StateChange` (`EventType = "PaymentPosted"`), and increment `RowVersion` — all in ONE transaction.
- `POST /api/v1/payments/{id}/post` MUST be the operator-driven completion/visibility seam and MUST NOT post an entry itself. Requires `finance.payment:post`. Returns 200, 404, 409. Resolution order: already `Posted` ⇒ idempotent 200; not `Confirmed` ⇒ `PAYMENT_NOT_CONFIRMED`; `Confirmed` but `JournalEntryId IS NULL` ⇒ the **re-enqueue recovery path** below, answered with `PAYMENT_POSTING_PENDING` (the posting-pending code, mirroring the `INVOICE_NOT_CONFIRMED` posting-pending semantics of SDD-INV-001 §2.5 but given its OWN code so a pending post is distinguishable from a wrong-state post); `Confirmed` AND linked ⇒ **UNREACHABLE defense-in-depth** — run the explicit period pre-check then confirm the `Posted` transition.
  - **Why the fourth branch is unreachable (labelled, not deleted).** `LinkPostedJournalEntryAsync` sets `JournalEntryId`, stamps `PostedAt`, and transitions `Confirmed → Posted` in ONE transaction, and §2.10 makes that link the ONLY writer of `JournalEntryId` — so `Confirmed` + non-null `JournalEntryId` is never a persisted state through the v1 paths. The branch, its §3.2 guard row, and its §6.2 test are retained as defense-in-depth against a future path that writes `JournalEntryId` without transitioning, exactly the way `PAYMENT_BASE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH` and the cancel-path `PAYMENT_HAS_ALLOCATIONS` are retained (§3.2).
- **Recovery — the operator post of a `Confirmed` AND UNLINKED payment MUST RE-ENQUEUE `PaymentConfirmedEvent` (MUST).** The endpoint MUST enqueue a fresh `PaymentConfirmedEvent` to the Payments transactional outbox carrying the SAME payload it would have carried at confirm (rebuilt from the persisted row), a **fresh `MessageId`** (so the Redis `SETNX` claim from the original delivery cannot short-circuit it inside the 7-day TTL) and the payment's **original stored `CorrelationId`** (the `CorrelationId` column, §2.16 — never the ambient request one, so the whole confirm→post→archive chain keeps one id), then return `PAYMENT_POSTING_PENDING` (409). The re-enqueue MUST NOT mutate the payment row: no state transition, no `PaymentStatusHistory` row, no `RowVersion` increment, no new document number, and no second audit `StateChange` — the payment is still `Confirmed` when the response is written, and `PaymentPosted` is audited by the back-event link path as before.
  - **This is safe ONLY because of the §2.5 source-document guard.** A re-published confirm is idempotent at the JOURNAL aggregate level: the consumer finds the already-`Posted` entry for `("Payment", PaymentId)`, posts nothing, and re-publishes `PaymentPostedEvent` for it — which is what actually unsticks a payment whose entry posted but whose back-event was lost. Without that guard this endpoint would post a duplicate entry on every operator retry, so the two requirements MUST ship together; neither is optional on its own.
  - Repeated operator retries are therefore bounded in effect: each adds one outbox message and at most one back-event, and never a second journal entry, a second `Posted` transition, or a second audit/history row.
  - The re-enqueue branch does NOT run the §2.9 payment period guard — §2.9 scopes that guard to confirm, reverse, and the unreachable `Confirmed`-AND-linked post branch. Postability is asserted where the entry is actually written, by the Journal service's own `GatewayPostingPeriodGuard` (`POSTING_PERIOD_CLOSED`); a closed period there fails the consume and leaves the payment `Confirmed`, i.e. exactly the state the operator started from, with no GL effect.
- **Failure & retry behavior (MUST).** A posting failure on the Journal side MUST be logged and rethrown as an exception so MassTransit retries (1s/5s/15s) and then dead-letters to `<queue>_error`; NO compensating event is published and the payment simply stays `Confirmed` until a retry succeeds. The same applies to a non-success `Result` in the back-event consumer. A `Confirmed`-but-unlinked payment is therefore a legitimate, observable intermediate state — it MUST NOT be auto-cancelled, auto-retried by a background job, or hidden from the list endpoint. Operators recover by fixing the cause (typically a missing/inactive posting rule or account) and either letting the retry/DLQ replay land or re-driving it through `POST /{id}/post` (the re-enqueue path above).
  - **Prerequisite: `CHG-FIX-006`, which ships in this SAME batch.** This retry-then-dead-letter contract is only true because the shared `IdempotencyFilter<T>` now RELEASES its Redis claim when the downstream pipe throws. Before that fix a failed consume left its own `finance:processed:{MessageId}` key claimed for the full 7-day TTL, the outer `UseMessageRetry` filter's redelivery was short-circuited as a "duplicate" of itself, and the message was silently ACKed — nothing ever reached `<queue>_error` (`src/Infrastructure/Messaging/Finance.Infrastructure.Messaging/MessagingServiceCollectionExtensions.cs` registers `UseMessageRetry` OUTSIDE `UseFinanceIdempotency`). Implementators MUST NOT re-derive this contract from pre-fix behavior.
  - **Where each idempotency guarantee lives (corrected).** Transport level: Redis `SETNX` on `MessageId` with a 7-day TTL, released on failure per `CHG-FIX-006` — this covers a fast retry of the SAME message on BOTH sides. Payments-side aggregate level: `LinkPostedJournalEntryAsync` returns a `Result.Success()` no-op when the payment is already `Posted` — that already-`Posted` guard belongs to the **back-event consumer**, NOT to the Journal-side posting consumer. Journal-side aggregate level: the `("Payment", PaymentId)` source-document check above — that, and not the already-`Posted` payment state, is what makes a redelivery past the dedupe window or a DLQ replay unable to post a second entry.
- **Deferred alternative (documented, not chosen):** synchronous optimistic posting at confirm (a Refit call to `POST /api/v1/posting/apply` through the gateway). Rejected for v1 for the same reasons SDD-INV-001 §2.5 rejected it — it couples the payment transaction to the Journal service's availability and breaks the async mesh. It MAY be revisited in a future `CHG-ENH-*`.

### 2.6 Update / delete / cancel (MUST)
- `PUT /api/v1/payments/{id}` MUST update a `Draft` payment only (method, counterparty, currency, amount, exchange rate, settlement account, payment date, bank reference). Requires `finance.payment:create`. Returns 200, 400, 404, 409.
  - An update against a `Confirmed`/`Posted`/`Cancelled`/`Reversed` payment MUST be rejected with `PAYMENT_POSTED_IMMUTABLE` (§2.10).
  - `DocumentType` MUST NOT be changeable after creation (it drives `Direction`, the sequence key, and the posting rule); a differing value MUST be rejected with `INVALID_PAYMENT_DOCUMENT_TYPE`.
  - The updated draft MUST recompute `BaseAmount` (§2.8) and re-run the settlement-account guard before persisting, and MUST write an audit `Update` row.
- `DELETE /api/v1/payments/{id}` MUST hard-delete a `Draft` payment only (it has no `DocumentNumber`, so no gapless number is consumed) and MUST write an audit `Delete` row (`AfterJson = "{\"deleted\":true}"`). Requires `finance.payment:create`. Returns 200, 404, 409. A non-`Draft` payment MUST yield `PAYMENT_POSTED_IMMUTABLE`.
- `POST /api/v1/payments/{id}/cancel` MUST cancel (void) a **`Draft` payment ONLY**. Requires `finance.payment:cancel`. Returns 200, 400, 404, 409.
  - A non-empty `Reason` MUST be supplied (cancellation is a SENSITIVE audit operation, SDD-AUDIT-001 — it voids an operator-entered cash document and keeps the row for audit instead of deleting it); a missing reason MUST yield `PAYMENT_CANCEL_REASON_REQUIRED`. The reason MUST be persisted to `CancellationReason` AND carried on both the audit row and the event.
  - Cancelling a `Confirmed`, `Posted`, `Cancelled`, or `Reversed` payment MUST be rejected with `INVALID_PAYMENT_STATE_TRANSITION`. `Confirmed → Cancelled` is deliberately NOT in `AllowedNextStates` (§2.1): the posting is already in flight, so a `Confirmed` payment is completed to `Posted` and then corrected by reversal (§2.7); a posted payment is reversed, never cancelled.
  - A `Draft` payment with `AllocatedAmount > 0` MUST be rejected with `PAYMENT_HAS_ALLOCATIONS` — the operator MUST deallocate first (SDD-PAY-002). Cancel MUST NEVER auto-release, auto-deallocate, or cascade-delete allocation rows; this is a hard block, not a compensating action. Because SDD-PAY-002 §2.5 admits only `Confirmed`/`Posted` payments as allocation targets (`PAYMENT_NOT_ALLOCATABLE`), a `Draft` can never carry allocations through the v1 paths — the guard is retained as **defense-in-depth**, like `PAYMENT_BASE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH` (§3.2).
  - A `Draft` has NULL `DocumentNumber`, so cancellation NEVER releases, reuses, or renumbers a gapless number — none was ever issued. `DocumentNumber` on a `Cancelled` row is therefore always NULL in v1.
  - Cancel MUST write an audit `StateChange` row (with the `Reason`) and enqueue `PaymentCancelledEvent` to the outbox.
- Optimistic concurrency MUST be enforced on every write path via the base64 `RowVersion` on the request; a malformed or stale token MUST yield `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION` (from `CommonErrorCodes`).

### 2.7 Reversal of a posted payment (MUST — the immutability-preserving correction)
- A `Posted` payment MUST NEVER be edited, deleted, or cancelled. To correct it the operator calls `POST /api/v1/payments/{id}/reverse`. Requires `finance.payment:reverse`. Returns 200, 400, 404, 409.
- Reverse MUST require a non-empty `Reason` (SENSITIVE, SDD-AUDIT-001); a missing reason MUST yield `PAYMENT_REVERSE_REASON_REQUIRED`.
- Reverse MUST be legal ONLY from `Posted`; any other source state MUST yield `INVALID_PAYMENT_STATE_TRANSITION`. A payment with `AllocatedAmount > 0` MUST be rejected with `PAYMENT_HAS_ALLOCATIONS` (deallocate first, SDD-PAY-002) so no allocation row is left pointing at a reversed payment.
- **Reverse MUST pre-check the fiscal period.** Before the transition, reverse MUST call `IPaymentPeriodGuard.EnsureOpenAsync(...)` (§2.9) over the **LINKED journal entry's `EntryDate`** — not over an unrelated "today" — surfacing the existing `PAYMENT_PERIOD_CLOSED` (409) on a closed period, on a date with no period, and on an unreachable Periods service (fail closed). No cross-service read is needed: the Journal-side consumer builds the entry with `EntryDate = message.PaymentDate` (§2.5), so the linked entry's `EntryDate` IS the payment's `PaymentDate` by construction; the guard is evaluated over `payment.PaymentDate` and that identity MUST be pinned by a test rather than assumed.
  - This pre-check is NOT redundant with the Journal service's own guard, which is **inert on the reversal path**: the shipped `JournalEntryService.ReverseInTransactionAsync` builds the offsetting entry with `EntryDate = original.EntryDate` (`BuildReversal`) and stamps it `Posted` directly via `StampPosted`, bypassing the workflow engine, while `Workflow/PostingPeriodWorkflowGuard.cs` returns success immediately unless `TargetState == nameof(JournalEntryStatus.Posted)` — and the transition performed on the original is `Posted → Reversed`. Without this endpoint-level pre-check, reversing a 2026-03-15 payment on 2026-05-02 would insert a `Posted` entry into a CLOSED March period, changing March's trial balance after close and invalidating a filed return.
  - **v1 does NOT re-date the reversing entry into the current open period.** The reversing entry keeps the original `EntryDate`, so a closed original period is a HARD BLOCK — the period MUST be reopened (SDD-FIN-004) before the reversal can proceed. Dating and arithmetic of the offsetting entry belong to SDD-FIN-002 and MUST NOT be changed here.
  - The deeper defect — that the Journal reversal path bypasses the period guard for EVERY document type, invoices included — is OUT OF SCOPE for this spec and is recorded as the change spec `CHG-FIX-004`. This pre-check is a partial mitigation for payments only; the generic hole remains.
- **Release-on-reverse is decided here, not left open.** Reverse (like cancel, §2.6) is a HARD BLOCK while `AllocatedAmount > 0`. This spec MUST NEVER auto-release, auto-deallocate, or cascade-delete allocation rows on cancel or reverse; the operator MUST deallocate through SDD-PAY-002 first, which is why the guard is a precondition rather than a compensating action. SDD-PAY-002 records this as a RESOLVED decision citing this section — there is no open question to settle jointly.
- Reverse MUST, in this order and in ONE transaction: (1) assert the non-empty `Reason`; (2) assert the source state is `Posted`; (3) assert `AllocatedAmount == 0.00`; (4) run the period pre-check above; (5) transition `Posted → Reversed` through the engine; (6) stamp `ReversedAt`; (7) append the status-history row (with the `Reason`); (8) write the audit `StateChange` row (`EventType = "PaymentReversed"`, with the `Reason`) — audit-first; (9) enqueue `PaymentReversedEvent` to the outbox. The header, `Amount`, `BaseAmount`, `DocumentNumber`, and `JournalEntryId` MUST NOT be mutated — ONLY the state flag, `ReversedAt`, `RowVersion`, and the appended history row.
- The GL correction MUST be a **sign-flipped new journal entry**, never an UPDATE: a Journal-side idempotent consumer of `PaymentReversedEvent` MUST reverse the linked entry through the shipped `IJournalEntryService.ReverseAsync` path (SDD-FIN-002 §2.6), which keeps the original entry with its `Reversed` flag and posts a new offsetting entry. This spec MUST NOT reimplement reversal arithmetic.
- **The consumer MUST read the entry BEFORE reversing it — the shipped signature demands a concurrency token.** The shipped member is `ReverseAsync(Guid id, ReverseJournalEntryRequest request, CancellationToken)`, and `ReverseJournalEntryRequest.RowVersion` is a `required string` that `JournalEntryService.ApplyConcurrencyToken` → `TryDecodeRowVersion` rejects when null, whitespace, or non-base64, returning `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION`. `PaymentReversedEvent` carries only `PaymentId`, `DocumentNumber`, `JournalEntryId`, and `Reason` (§2.14), so the consumer holds no token. It MUST therefore, in order: (1) LOAD the entry identified by `message.JournalEntryId`; (2) take its base64 `RowVersion` (the form `JournalEntryDto.RowVersion` already exposes); (3) call `ReverseAsync(message.JournalEntryId, new ReverseJournalEntryRequest { Reason = message.Reason, RowVersion = <that token> }, ct)`.
- **The reversal consumer MUST have the same KIND of aggregate-level idempotency §2.5 gives the posting consumer** (there, an already-`Posted` entry for the `("Payment", PaymentId)` source document; here, an already-`Reversed` linked entry). A linked entry already in `Reversed` MUST be a `Result.Success()` NO-OP and MUST NOT be passed to `ReverseAsync`, which returns `INVALID_JOURNAL_STATE_TRANSITION` for any non-`Posted` entry. Without that no-op a redelivery after the 7-day Redis `SETNX` TTL, or a DLQ replay, would permanently self-renew the dead letter — the transport-level `MessageId` dedupe alone is not sufficient.
- No new document number is allocated on reversal (the payment keeps its number; the offsetting entry gets its own journal-entry number from SDD-FIN-002). The reversing entry's id is NOT stored on the payment in v1 — it is discoverable through the Journal's own reversal linkage; adding a `ReversalJournalEntryId` column would be additive (§5).

### 2.8 Amounts, FX & the settlement account (MUST — SDD-ACCT-001 / SDD-FIN-005)
- `Amount` MUST be `decimal` / `DECIMAL(18,2)` and strictly `> 0` — a payment is always a positive cash movement; direction is carried by `DocumentType`/`Direction`, NEVER by a negative amount. `ExchangeRate` MUST be `decimal` / `DECIMAL(18,6)` and `> 0`. `double`/`float` MUST NEVER appear on any payment code path.
- `ExchangeRate` MUST equal `1.000000` exactly when `CurrencyCode == BaseCurrencyCode`; otherwise `INVALID_PAYMENT_EXCHANGE_RATE`.
- `BaseAmount` MUST be computed by the service as `ICountryStrategy.ApplyTaxRounding(Amount × ExchangeRate)` — the rounding mode MUST NOT be inlined in core code (SDD-CTRY-001). A client-supplied `BaseAmount` MUST be ignored; the recomputed value MUST reconcile to the cent or the operation MUST fail with `PAYMENT_BASE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`. The computation MUST live in a single-responsibility `PaymentAmountCalculator` (mirroring `InvoiceTotalsCalculator`).
- `UnallocatedAmount = Amount − AllocatedAmount` MUST be COMPUTED (a `[NotMapped]` property surfaced on `PaymentDto`), never a stored column and never a persisted computed column in v1. `AllocatedAmount` MUST default to `0.00` and MUST be maintained EXCLUSIVELY by SDD-PAY-002 — no endpoint in this spec writes it.
- `SettlementAccountId` MUST be validated through a `ISettlementAccountReader` seam implemented by `GatewaySettlementAccountReader` over the Refit `IAccountReadClient` (`GET /api/v1/accounts/{id}` through the gateway, with `CorrelationIdDelegatingHandler` → `BearerTokenForwardingHandler` → `AddStandardResilienceHandler`). Resolution: missing/404 ⇒ `PAYMENT_SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`; found but `IsActive == false` ⇒ `PAYMENT_SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNT_INACTIVE`; unreachable ⇒ **fail closed** with `PAYMENT_SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`, matching the shipped `GatewayReferenceDataReader` convention (financial safety over availability).
- **`OperationCanceledException` MUST be rethrown, never folded into the fail-closed branch** — a caller that abandoned the request MUST NOT be told the settlement account is missing, and a cancelled confirm MUST surface as a cancellation rather than as a validation failure that could be misread as a data problem. §2.9 states the same rule for the period guard; both readers MUST behave identically. Shipped in `GatewaySettlementAccountReader.EnsureUsableAsync` (`src/Interfaces/Payments/Finance.Payments.API/Services/GatewaySettlementAccountReader.cs:69-72`, a dedicated `catch (OperationCanceledException) { throw; }` ahead of the general `catch`) and pinned by `EnsureUsable_Cancelled_RethrowsOperationCanceled` (§6.3).

### 2.9 Fiscal-period guard (MUST — SDD-FIN-004, reusing the fulfilled seam)
- `IPaymentPeriodGuard.EnsureOpenAsync(DateTimeOffset paymentDate, CancellationToken ct)` MUST be implemented by `GatewayPaymentPeriodGuard` over the Refit `IPeriodReadClient` (`GET /api/v1/periods/by-date`) — the SAME shipped lookup SDD-FIN-004 §2.7 built to fulfil the SDD-FIN-002 seam. It MUST be gateway-backed from day one; an always-open stub MUST exist for tests ONLY and MUST NOT be registered in production (this deliberately does NOT repeat the Invoices service's `AlwaysOpenInvoicePeriodGuard` production registration).
- Resolution: period `Open` ⇒ `Result.Success()`; period `Closed` ⇒ `PAYMENT_PERIOD_CLOSED`; no period for the date (`NO_PERIOD_FOR_DATE`/404) ⇒ `PAYMENT_PERIOD_CLOSED`; Periods service unreachable or any non-404 upstream error ⇒ `PAYMENT_PERIOD_CLOSED` (**fail closed**). `OperationCanceledException` MUST be rethrown.
- **On `Draft → Confirmed` the guard runs TWICE, deliberately — this is the shipped platform pattern, not a duplication to remove.** The service MUST call `IPaymentPeriodGuard.EnsureOpenAsync(payment.PaymentDate, ct)` as an explicit pre-check BEFORE the transaction opens, so a closed period consumes NO gapless sequence value, writes NO audit row and enqueues NO outbox message (§2.4 step 1 — a burnt number can never be recycled, so the check cannot be left until the transition); and `PaymentPeriodWorkflowGuard` MUST ALSO run inside `IWorkflowEngine<Payment>` as the SDD-INFRA-007 chain guard, so no future caller can reach the transition without it. This mirrors the shipped `Finance.Invoices.API` exactly (`InvoiceService.RunConfirmGuardsAsync` calls the guard directly AND `InvoicePeriodWorkflowGuard` is registered as a chain validator). Both reads fail closed identically, so correctness does not depend on the order; the cost is a second `GET /api/v1/periods/by-date` round trip and a second consumption of the resilience budget on every successful confirm. Diverging from the Invoices pattern to save that round trip is out of scope here — raise a `CHG-REFAC-*` against BOTH services if it is ever worth doing.
- The guard MUST run on `Draft → Confirmed` (through both layers above) and as an explicit pre-check on `POST /{id}/reverse` (§2.7, over the linked entry's `EntryDate`) — the two REACHABLE operator paths. It MUST also be wired as an explicit pre-check on the `Confirmed`-AND-linked branch of `POST /{id}/post`, which is **UNREACHABLE defense-in-depth** (§2.5): `Confirmed` + non-null `JournalEntryId` is never a persisted state, so that pre-check can never fire through the v1 paths.
- **`IPeriodReadClient` is a per-service copy too.** The shipped contract lives in `namespace Finance.Journal.API.Interfaces` (`src/Interfaces/Journal/Finance.Journal.API/Interfaces/IPeriodReadClient.cs`) and is private to the Journal service; "reusing the fulfilled seam" means reusing the shipped **Periods HTTP endpoint**, not the C# interface. `Finance.Payments.API` MUST declare its own `IPeriodReadClient` under `Finance.Payments.API/Interfaces/` with the same handler chain (§2.8) — never a project reference to `Finance.Journal.API`.

> **The back-event link path MUST NOT re-run the period guard.** By the time `PaymentPostedEvent` arrives, the Journal service has ALREADY asserted postability of the same date through its own `GatewayPostingPeriodGuard` (`POSTING_PERIOD_CLOSED`, SDD-FIN-004 §2.7) — the entry could not have been posted otherwise. Re-checking inside `LinkPostedJournalEntryAsync` would let a period closed between confirm and link poison the consumer into a permanent retry/DLQ loop while the GL already holds the entry, leaving the payment `Confirmed` forever. The guard is therefore scoped to the operator-initiated paths only — two reachable (confirm, reverse) plus the unreachable defense-in-depth pre-check on the operator post (§2.5) — and `PaymentPeriodWorkflowGuard` stays inert for `TargetState == nameof(PaymentStatus.Posted)`.

### 2.10 Immutability of confirmed/posted payments (MUST — SDD-AUDIT-001)
- A `Confirmed`, `Posted`, `Cancelled`, or `Reversed` payment's header, amounts, `DocumentNumber`, and `SettlementAccountId` MUST NEVER be UPDATEd. The permitted mutations are the state-flag transitions defined above (confirm→post link, post→reversed flag), each with its `RowVersion` increment, status-history row, and audit row — plus the single `AllocatedAmount` carve-out below. `CancellationReason` is NOT in that set: cancel is legal from `Draft` only (§2.1/§2.6), so a confirmed-or-later payment never has it written.
- **Narrow carve-out — `AllocatedAmount` (SDD-PAY-002).** `AllocatedAmount` is settlement bookkeeping ABOUT the payment, not part of the issued document, so it is NOT covered by the immutability rule above and MAY be written after confirm EXCLUSIVELY by the SDD-PAY-002 allocate/deallocate paths (SDD-PAY-002 §2.4 / §2.6). No endpoint in THIS spec writes it. The write performs NO state transition and appends NO `PaymentStatusHistory` row; its `RowVersion` increment is the allocation path's own optimistic-concurrency guard. Together with the confirm→post link (`JournalEntryId` / `PostedAt`) and the `Reversed` flag + `ReversedAt`, `AllocatedAmount` forms the **COMPLETE set of columns a confirmed-or-later payment may ever have written** — anything else is a defect. (This mirrors the way the SDD-INV-001 settlement amendment carved `SettledAmount` out of posted-invoice immutability.)
- `PUT`/`DELETE` against a non-`Draft` payment MUST be rejected with `PAYMENT_POSTED_IMMUTABLE`. There is NO hard delete of a confirmed-or-later payment, ever.
- `PaymentStatusHistory` MUST be append-only: rows are INSERTed on every transition and MUST NEVER be UPDATEd or DELETEd (except by the FK cascade when a `Draft` — which has no history rows — is deleted).

### 2.18 Edge cases (MUST)
- **Confirm a non-`Draft` payment.** `POST .../confirm` on a `Confirmed`/`Posted`/`Cancelled`/`Reversed` payment MUST return `PAYMENT_NOT_DRAFT` — never a second gapless number, never a duplicate `PaymentConfirmedEvent`.
- **Confirm with a zero or negative amount.** MUST return `INVALID_PAYMENT_AMOUNT` before any sequence value is consumed.
- **Confirm into a closed period.** MUST return `PAYMENT_PERIOD_CLOSED` before any number is allocated; the same code MUST be returned when the date has no period at all and when the Periods service is unreachable (fail closed, §2.9).
- **Confirm a prior-year payment.** A payment whose `PaymentDate.Year` differs from the confirm-clock year MUST return `PAYMENT_DATE_YEAR_MISMATCH` (409) before any sequence value is consumed — never a next-year number on a prior-year document (§2.4).
- **Create or update with a future `PaymentDate`.** MUST return `INVALID_PAYMENT_DATE` (§3.1) — cash cannot be recorded as having moved before it moves, and a future date would also draw a number from a series the books have not reached.
- **Post while the handshake is still in flight.** `POST .../post` on a `Confirmed` payment whose `JournalEntryId` is NULL MUST re-enqueue `PaymentConfirmedEvent` (fresh `MessageId`, the payment's stored `CorrelationId`) and return `PAYMENT_POSTING_PENDING` (409) — NOT `PAYMENT_NOT_CONFIRMED`, and NOT a silent 200. The payment row MUST NOT be mutated by the call (§2.5).
- **Duplicate posting back-event (replay).** A redundant `PaymentPostedEvent` for an already-`Posted` payment MUST be a no-op — never a second `Posted` transition, never a second audit/history row, never an overwritten `JournalEntryId`.
- **Redelivered `PaymentConfirmedEvent` past the dedupe window (or a DLQ replay).** The Journal-side consumer MUST find the already-`Posted` entry for `("Payment", PaymentId)`, post NOTHING, re-publish `PaymentPostedEvent` for that existing entry, and succeed — never a second journal entry. The transport-level `MessageId` dedupe MUST NOT be relied on for this: its Redis claim expires after 7 days and a DLQ replay is a deliberate operator action (§2.5). The UNIQUE FILTERED index `IX_JournalEntries_SourceDocument` is the DB backstop if the check is ever bypassed.
- **A source document whose entry was legitimately REVERSED.** The original entry leaves the `Posted` filter, so no `Posted` entry exists for the pair and the §2.5 guard would permit a fresh post. That is not a silent duplicate: the payment itself is then `Reversed`, so the resulting `PaymentPostedEvent` MUST fail `LinkPostedJournalEntryAsync` with `PAYMENT_NOT_CONFIRMED` and dead-letter for operator attention rather than mutating a reversed payment back to `Posted`.
- **Posting back-event for an unknown or cancelled payment.** MUST fail with `PAYMENT_NOT_FOUND` / `PAYMENT_NOT_CONFIRMED` and rethrow so MassTransit retries and finally dead-letters (prerequisite `CHG-FIX-006`, §2.5) — the payment MUST NOT be silently mutated into `Posted` from an illegal state.
- **Non-base-currency payment.** A payment in a non-base currency MUST store `Amount`/`ExchangeRate`/`BaseAmount` and MUST post the TRANSACTIONAL `Amount` in `CurrencyCode` (§2.5) so its control-account leg is booked in the same currency as the invoice leg; the realized FX difference against a settled invoice MUST NOT be computed or posted here (SDD-PAY-002 / SDD-FIN-005).
- **Base-currency payment with a rate other than 1.** MUST return `INVALID_PAYMENT_EXCHANGE_RATE`.
- **Cancelling a `Confirmed` payment.** MUST return `INVALID_PAYMENT_STATE_TRANSITION` — cancel is legal from `Draft` only (§2.1). The posting is already in flight, so a confirmed payment is completed to `Posted` and corrected by reversal; there is no void-after-confirm path in v1.
- **A payment stuck in `Confirmed` because the Journal never posted.** Typically a missing/inactive posting rule or account, or a dead-lettered message. This is an OPERATIONAL concern, not a data-integrity one, and it has a REAL recovery path: the operator fixes the cause and calls `POST /{id}/post`, which **re-enqueues `PaymentConfirmedEvent`** to the outbox with a fresh `MessageId` and the payment's stored `CorrelationId` and answers `PAYMENT_POSTING_PENDING` (§2.5). Two outcomes, both safe: no entry was ever posted ⇒ the consumer now posts one and the back-event links the payment; the entry WAS posted but its back-event was lost ⇒ the `("Payment", PaymentId)` source-document guard makes the consumer post nothing and re-publish `PaymentPostedEvent` for the existing entry. The call MUST be repeatable without producing a second journal entry or a second gapless number, and the payment MUST NOT be cancelled, auto-cancelled, hidden from the list, or force-transitioned to `Posted`. `CHG-FIX-006` is the prerequisite that also makes the plain retry/DLQ route work (§2.5).
- **Cancelling or reversing an allocated payment.** MUST return `PAYMENT_HAS_ALLOCATIONS`; the operator deallocates through SDD-PAY-002 first. On the cancel path this is defense-in-depth only — a `Draft` cannot be an allocation target (§2.6).
- **Editing or deleting a confirmed-or-later payment.** `PUT`/`DELETE` MUST return `PAYMENT_POSTED_IMMUTABLE`; a `Posted` payment is corrected only by reversal.
- **Reversing a non-`Posted` payment.** MUST return `INVALID_PAYMENT_STATE_TRANSITION`; reversing an already-`Reversed` payment MUST NOT produce a second offsetting entry.
- **Reversing a payment whose original period has closed.** `POST .../reverse` MUST return `PAYMENT_PERIOD_CLOSED` (409) — the reversing entry keeps the original `EntryDate`, so v1 does NOT re-date it into the current open period; the period MUST be reopened first (§2.7, SDD-FIN-004).
- **Replayed `PaymentReversedEvent` on the Journal side.** A redelivery whose linked entry is already `Reversed` MUST be a `Result.Success()` no-op — never a second call into `ReverseAsync` (which would fail with `INVALID_JOURNAL_STATE_TRANSITION` and self-renew the dead letter, §2.7).
- **Concurrent confirm of the same draft.** Two simultaneous confirms — one MUST win; the other MUST fail with `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION` (RowVersion mismatch), and exactly ONE gapless number MUST be consumed.
- **Settlement account deactivated after confirm.** A payment already `Confirmed`/`Posted` MUST NOT be invalidated retroactively; the account check runs on create/update/confirm only.
- **Inactive/missing posting-rule template.** If `PAYMENT_CUSTOMER_RECEIPT`/`PAYMENT_SUPPLIER_PAYMENT` is absent or inactive, the Journal consumer MUST fail with `POSTING_RULE_NOT_FOUND` and retry/DLQ (prerequisite `CHG-FIX-006`, §2.5); the payment MUST remain `Confirmed` and MUST stay visible in the list, and re-driving `POST /{id}/post` after the rule is seeded MUST complete the handshake (§2.5).

**Already covered by `DefaultErrorCodeToStatusMap` — MUST NOT be re-mapped.** `PAYMENT_DUPLICATE_DOCUMENT_NUMBER` resolves to **409** through the default map's `Contains("DUPLICATE")` test (`src/Infrastructure/Web/Finance.Infrastructure.Web/ErrorMapping/DefaultErrorCodeToStatusMap.cs`, `IsConflictFamily` — a `Contains`, NOT a suffix match, so the code needs no explicit entry); `PAYMENT_SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNT_INACTIVE` resolves to **409** through the `*_INACTIVE` suffix; `PAYMENT_NOT_FOUND` / `PAYMENT_SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND` resolve to **404** through the `*_NOT_FOUND` suffix; `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION` resolves to **409** through `CONCURRENT_*`; and every validation code falls through to **400**.

**Needs an explicit entry — the EIGHT codes from THIS spec.** `PAYMENT_NOT_DRAFT`, `PAYMENT_NOT_CONFIRMED`, `PAYMENT_POSTING_PENDING`, `PAYMENT_POSTED_IMMUTABLE`, `INVALID_PAYMENT_STATE_TRANSITION`, `PAYMENT_PERIOD_CLOSED`, `PAYMENT_HAS_ALLOCATIONS`, and `PAYMENT_DATE_YEAR_MISMATCH` are state/numbering conflicts → **409** and match NONE of the default patterns, so they would silently fall through to 400. A `PaymentErrorCodeToStatusMap` MUST be added and registered via `services.Replace(...)` carrying them and delegating any code it does not name to a private `new DefaultErrorCodeToStatusMap()`.

**The map is SHARED with SDD-PAY-002 — it MUST NOT be built from this list alone.** `PaymentErrorCodeToStatusMap` is the ONE domain map this service registers, and SDD-PAY-002 §4 adds its own EIGHT 409 codes to it (`PAYMENT_NOT_ALLOCATABLE`, `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_INVOICE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_PAYMENT`, `PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_OUTSTANDING`, and the four `*_MISMATCH` codes — direction, counterparty, currency, control account). Both specs ship in this batch, so **once both are implemented the map MUST carry SIXTEEN codes** (8 here + 8 from SDD-PAY-002); the §2.5 source-document guard and the §2.5 re-enqueue recovery add NO client-visible code of their own (the recovery reuses `PAYMENT_POSTING_PENDING`), and SDD-PAY-003's codes are all validation codes that correctly stay **400**. Building the map verbatim from this section alone would leave SDD-PAY-002's codes falling through to the default map — which matches none of their patterns — returning 400 where SDD-PAY-002 documents 409, a client-visible contract break. SDD-PAY-002 §4 states the reciprocal obligation.

`INVALID_PAYMENT_STATE_TRANSITION` is the Payment-domain alias surfaced to clients for the workflow engine's generic `INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`/`STATE_NOT_REGISTERED` (SDD-INFRA-008 §4); the service MUST translate the engine's failure code, and MUST surface a `WORKFLOW_GUARD_FAILED` with a non-null `Detail` as the guard's own code (`PAYMENT_PERIOD_CLOSED`, `PAYMENT_HAS_ALLOCATIONS`), mirroring SDD-FIN-002 §4 and SDD-INV-001 §4.

**Cross-service note (SDD-FIN-006).** `POSTING_RULE_NOT_FOUND`, `POSTING_RULE_UNBALANCED`, `MISSING_POSTING_AMOUNT`, and `POSTING_PERIOD_CLOSED` are raised on the JOURNAL side of the handshake and MUST NOT be redefined in `PaymentErrorCodes`. They never reach a payment API caller — they surface as a failed consume, a retry, and finally a DLQ message, with the payment left `Confirmed` (§2.5).

**Frontend obligation — DISCHARGED in Batch 17 (locale keys only; no payments views yet).** Every code above MUST have a matching `errors.<CODE>` entry in BOTH `frontend/src/shared/i18n/locales/en.ts` and `bg.ts` (SDD-UI-001). All **24** codes this spec owns shipped in both locales ahead of any payments view, pinned by `frontend/src/shared/i18n/paymentErrorCodes.test.ts`, which asserts EN presence, BG presence, a non-empty message per code, that no message is a raw key path, whole-`errors`-group EN/BG parity, and Cyrillic text in BG. `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION` and `PAGE_SIZE_TOO_LARGE` pre-existed on the frontend and MUST NOT be duplicated. The payments VIEWS remain deferred to SDD-UI-FIN-002, which is not yet authored.

## 5. Versioning Notes

`/api/v1/payments/*` is the v1 surface: `GET` (list), `GET /{id}`, `POST` (create draft), `PUT /{id}` (update draft), `DELETE /{id}` (delete draft), `POST /{id}/confirm`, `POST /{id}/post`, `POST /{id}/cancel`, `POST /{id}/reverse`.

- **v1 — Initial specification (Phase 5, Payments).** The `Payment` aggregate (two document types, derived `AR`/`AP` direction, three methods); the new `Finance.Payments.API` (port 6006, database `finance_payments`, schema `payments`) and `Finance.Payments.DBModel`; `Draft → Confirmed → Posted` (+ `Cancelled` from `Draft` only, `Reversed` from `Posted`) via `IWorkflowEngine<Payment>`; gapless country-formatted numbering at confirm via `ISequenceGenerator` + `ICountryStrategy`, with the number's year pinned to the confirm clock and enforced by `PAYMENT_DATE_YEAR_MISMATCH`; the gateway-backed SDD-FIN-004 period guard on confirm and reverse (plus the unreachable defense-in-depth pre-check on the operator post, §2.5); settlement-account existence/active validation with the dormant postable seam; audit-first → outbox `PaymentConfirmedEvent` / `PaymentCancelledEvent` / `PaymentReversedEvent`; the event-driven Confirm→Post handshake with the Journal service including `PAYMENT_POSTING_PENDING` and its confirm-event re-enqueue recovery path; the `JournalEntry` source-document duplicate-post backstop; posting of the TRANSACTIONAL amount in the payment currency; reversal by sign-flipped journal entry; `Confirmed`/`Posted` immutable; no caching.
- **`ICountryStrategy` interface growth (breaking to the interface, per SDD-CTRY-001 §5).** This spec adds `string GenerateDocumentNumber(PaymentDocumentType documentType, long sequenceValue)` as a SECOND, payment-typed overload alongside the shipped invoice-typed member — the existing signature MUST NOT be changed, so the growth is additive for every existing caller. `BulgariaStrategy` MUST implement it as `{prefix}-{year}-{seq:D6}` with the **Latin** prefixes `RCT` (`CustomerReceipt`) and `PAY` (`SupplierPayment`) — i.e. exactly `RCT-{yyyy}-{nnnnnn}` and `PAY-{yyyy}-{nnnnnn}` — because that is the format `docs/infrastructure/SDD-INFRA-003-sequence-generation.md` §2.1 (already `Implemented`) documents and the shipped `SequenceDefinitions.cs` / `SequenceKeys.cs` already set (`Prefix = "RCT"` / `Prefix = "PAY"`, padding 6, yearly reset). **NO amendment to SDD-INFRA-003 and NO change to `SequenceDefinitions`/`SequenceKeys` is part of this batch.** The overload deliberately takes NO date — `{yyyy}` comes from the ambient clock, exactly as the shipped invoice-typed member does (`DateTimeOffset.UtcNow.Year`) and exactly as the shipped `SequenceGenerator` composes its yearly reset key (`_timeProvider.GetUtcNow()`); passing a date in would be an SDD-INFRA-003-coordinated change this batch does not own, so the year is instead pinned by the §2.2 confirm guard. Method- or Cyrillic-specific numbering is a deferred open item (§7).
- **`BulgariaStrategy.GetDefaultPostingRules()` growth (additive).** `PAYMENT_CUSTOMER_RECEIPT` and `PAYMENT_SUPPLIER_PAYMENT` are added, plus the bundled `CREDIT_NOTE`/`DEBIT_NOTE` fix whose selectors, sides, and `PostingAmountSource` values are specified line-by-line in §2.13. Adding a template is additive; changing the account selectors or **sides** of an existing seeded rule is breaking and requires a `CHG-ENH-*` plus a re-seeding plan (the seeder never overwrites) — which is precisely why the two note templates carry an accountant sign-off gate (§7) and a sides-asserting test (§6.4) rather than shipping as an unverified copy of `SALE_INVOICE`.
- **`JournalEntry` source-document link (additive, SDD-FIN-002 / SDD-FIN-006 surfaces).** `SourceDocumentType` (`NVARCHAR(40) NULL`) + `SourceDocumentId` (`UNIQUEIDENTIFIER NULL`) on `journal.JournalEntries`, the UNIQUE FILTERED index `IX_JournalEntries_SourceDocument` (both non-null AND `Status = 'Posted'`), a new `<timestamp>_AddJournalEntrySourceDocument` migration, two nullable non-`required` properties on `ApplyPostingRuleRequest` and `CreateJournalEntryRequest`, and a new `IJournalEntryService.FindPostedBySourceDocumentAsync` member (§2.5). All additive: existing rows keep NULLs and are exempt from the index, existing callers of the two records compile and behave unchanged, and no shipped API contract changes. Interface growth on `IJournalEntryService` is breaking to implementors of that interface only (one production implementation plus test doubles), the same way this section's `ICountryStrategy` growth is. Adding a NEW source-document tag value is additive; changing an existing tag value (`"Payment"`, `"Invoice"`) after any entry has been stamped is BREAKING — it would orphan the dedupe key of every stamped entry — and requires a `CHG-ENH-*` plus a data fix-up.
- **Behavior change to the SHIPPED `InvoiceConfirmedEventConsumer` (hardening, not a contract change).** It gains the same stamping and already-`Posted` no-op (§2.5). No event payload, endpoint, or status code changes; the only observable difference is that a redelivered `InvoiceConfirmedEvent` past the dedupe window no longer produces a second journal entry. Recorded in the SDD-INV-001 amendment's change log.
- **`POST /{id}/post` re-enqueue (additive to an existing endpoint).** The endpoint keeps its route, request shape, permission, and documented outcome set (200/404/409) and still returns `PAYMENT_POSTING_PENDING` for a `Confirmed`-and-unlinked payment; the added side effect is one outbox message (§2.5). Because the response contract is unchanged, this is additive for clients; removing it later would break the documented recovery path in §2.18 and needs a `CHG-ENH-*`.
- **Deferred (future versions / specs):**
  - **Allocation, settlement status, and the realized-FX seam** — SDD-PAY-002 (additive; it only writes `AllocatedAmount` and its own tables, no change to this lifecycle).
  - **AP/AR aging, open items, counterparty balances** — SDD-PAY-003 (read-only inside this service; no new tables, no change here).
  - **Refund document types and a `Compensation`/offset method** — a future `CHG-FEAT-*`; both would be breaking enum changes (new persisted string values + workflow/posting-rule mapping + migration per SDD-INFRA-008 §5).
  - **Automatic FX rate lookup and GL posting of realized FX differences** — SDD-FIN-005; the same spec owns making the general ledger genuinely multi-currency, which is what would let the posted `Base*Amount` columns hold a real base-currency translation instead of the transactional amount (the platform limitation recorded in §7). v1 posts the transactional amount in the payment's currency (§2.5) and takes the rate on the request.
  - **A void-after-confirm ("cancel a `Confirmed` payment") path** — deliberately absent from v1 (§2.1). Adding it would be a breaking change to the transition semantics and would require a compensating-reversal design for the in-flight posting, plus a `CHG-ENH-*`.
  - **`ReversalJournalEntryId` on the payment** — additive column + DTO field, a future `CHG-ENH-*`.
  - **Bank statement import / reconciliation and payment-file export** — a later phase; both consume this aggregate without changing it.
  - **Counterparty enrichment for display** — SDD-INT-WH-002 (read-only enrichment; additive).
  - **The payments frontend** — `SDD-UI-FIN-002`, not yet authored. The `errors.<CODE>` locale keys were the only obligation this spec carried and they shipped in Batch 17 (§4); the views themselves remain deferred.
- Adding an event field or a response field is additive; changing the state set/transition semantics, the `Direction` derivation, an enum's persisted string values, or the document-number format is breaking and requires `/api/v2/` + a `CHG-ENH-*` (+ an enum migration / НАП-stability review for number formats).

## 7. Resolved Decisions & Open Items

### Resolved
- **One aggregate, two document types.** `Payment` discriminated by `DocumentType` (`CustomerReceipt`/`SupplierPayment`) with `Direction` (`AR`/`AP`) derived and frozen; header `payments.Payments` + append-only `payments.PaymentStatusHistory`. `Payments.Id` = `UNIQUEIDENTIFIER`/`NEWSEQUENTIALID()` (event-exposed and externally referenced by allocations and journal entries), `CorrelationId` column, `DECIMAL(18,2)` amounts / `DECIMAL(18,6)` rates, `DATETIMEOFFSET` timestamps, outbox tables in the same database (Plan Appendix A).
- **`PaymentDirection` mirrors `InvoiceDirection` value-for-value (`AP = 1`, `AR = 2`).** Numeric parity is deliberate so SDD-PAY-002's direction-match guard compares the two enums without a translation table; the member set is identical.
- **New service, not an extension of Invoices.** `Finance.Payments.API` (port **6006**, database `finance_payments`, schema `payments`) + `Finance.Payments.DBModel`, following the shipped Invoices layout and composition order file-for-file. Payments own a distinct lifecycle, distinct RBAC surface, and a distinct sub-ledger; folding them into Invoices would violate the one-database-per-service rule for the allocation projection SDD-PAY-002 needs.
- **Numbering at confirm only, in the already-documented Latin format.** Raw gapless counter from `ISequenceGenerator.NextValueAsync` on the EXISTING `RCT`/`PAY` keys, formatted by a new payment-typed `ICountryStrategy.GenerateDocumentNumber` overload into exactly `RCT-{yyyy}-{nnnnnn}` / `PAY-{yyyy}-{nnnnnn}` — the format SDD-INFRA-003 §2.1 already documents and the shipped `SequenceDefinitions`/`SequenceKeys` already carry, so this batch changes NO sequence artifact and needs NO SDD-INFRA-003 amendment. A draft has NULL `DocumentNumber`, and because cancel is `Draft`-only (§2.1) a `Cancelled` payment never held a number — so no number is ever released, recycled, or reassigned.
- **The document number's year is pinned to the confirm clock, and a mismatch is rejected.** Both the shipped `BulgariaStrategy.GenerateDocumentNumber` (`DateTimeOffset.UtcNow.Year`) and the shipped `SequenceGenerator`'s yearly reset key (`_timeProvider.GetUtcNow()` → `SequenceKeyComposer.PeriodSegment`) derive the year from the ambient clock, so the number and the counter series always agree with each other — but not necessarily with `PaymentDate`. Rather than change the strategy signature (an SDD-INFRA-003-coordinated change this batch does not own), the §2.2 step-4 confirm guard rejects `PaymentDate.Year ≠` confirm-clock year with `PAYMENT_DATE_YEAR_MISMATCH` (409). Combined with the §3.1 "not in the future" upper bound on `PaymentDate`, that makes a year-straddling number impossible.
- **Posting handshake (chosen): event-driven with a dedicated back-event.** Confirm publishes `PaymentConfirmedEvent` via the outbox; the Journal service consumes it, posts through `IPostingEngine.ApplyAsync` (SDD-FIN-006), and publishes back the dedicated `PaymentPostedEvent` (NOT the generic `JournalEntryPostedEvent`, which is multi-purpose and already consumed by EventLog); the Payments-side idempotent consumer links `JournalEntryId` and moves `Confirmed → Posted`. Payments NEVER post an entry themselves. Synchronous optimistic posting is the documented, deferred alternative (§2.5).
- **Posting-pending gets its own code.** `PAYMENT_POSTING_PENDING` (409) instead of overloading `PAYMENT_NOT_CONFIRMED` the way SDD-INV-001 overloaded `INVOICE_NOT_CONFIRMED` — a client polling for completion can then distinguish "wrong state" from "wait and retry" without parsing `detail`.
- **Duplicate posting is prevented at the JOURNAL aggregate, not by transport dedupe.** `JournalEntry` gains additive `SourceDocumentType`/`SourceDocumentId` columns plus a UNIQUE FILTERED index over the pair for `Posted` entries, and the Journal-side consumer no-ops (and re-publishes the back-event) when a `Posted` entry already exists for `("Payment", PaymentId)` (§2.5). Redis `SETNX` on `MessageId` was rejected as the sole guard: the claim expires after 7 days and a `finance-journal_error` replay is a deliberate operator action, so a redelivery would post a SECOND entry — cash `503` overstated and `411` understated, trial balance still balanced, sub-ledger still reporting one settlement, and `IX_Payments_JournalEntryId UNIQUE` blocking only a second LINK. The pattern is copied from the shipped `Invoice.SourceDocumentId`/`SourceDocumentType` + `IX_Invoices_SourceDocument` Warehouse dedupe, and the SHIPPED `InvoiceConfirmedEventConsumer` — which had the identical exposure — is hardened the same way in this batch.
- **Also corrected here: where each idempotency guarantee lives.** The already-`Posted` no-op is the PAYMENTS-side back-event consumer's guard (`LinkPostedJournalEntryAsync`). The Journal-side posting consumer's guard is the source-document check above. Conflating the two — attributing an already-`Posted` no-op to the posting consumer — would make the replay-safety claim false, because that consumer never reads the payment row (§2.5).
- **A stuck `Confirmed` payment has a REAL recovery path, and it is `POST /{id}/post`.** That endpoint re-enqueues `PaymentConfirmedEvent` (fresh `MessageId`, the payment's stored `CorrelationId`) without mutating the payment row, then answers `PAYMENT_POSTING_PENDING` (§2.5). Returning 409 forever was rejected: the payment carries a legally issued gapless number and MUST NOT be strandable with no GL entry, and cancel is `Draft`-only by design. The re-enqueue is safe ONLY because of the source-document guard — the two requirements ship together. Rebuilding the payment's `Amount`/`CurrencyCode`/`PostingRuleKey` payload from the persisted row (rather than storing the original event) is deliberate: `Confirmed` amounts are immutable (§2.10), so the rebuilt payload is byte-identical in every field the Journal side reads.
- **Transactional-currency posting in v1 — symmetry with the shipped invoice consumer wins.** The Journal-side amount context sends `Amounts[Gross] = Amount` in `CurrencyCode`, exactly as the shipped `InvoiceConfirmedEventConsumer` sends the transactional `NetTotal`/`TaxTotal`/`GrossTotal` in `message.CurrencyCode`. Posting `BaseAmount` in `BaseCurrencyCode` instead was rejected: the two legs of the SAME control account would then be booked in different currencies, leaving a permanent residual on `411`/`401` for every foreign-currency invoice+receipt pair while SDD-PAY-002 derives `Settled` and SDD-PAY-003 reports `Outstanding = 0.00`. No growth of `PostingAmountSource` is required either way. `BaseAmount` + `ExchangeRate` remain on the payment row for reporting and the SDD-PAY-002 realized-FX computation.
- **Gateway-backed period guard from day one, on two operator paths (confirm, reverse).** `GatewayPaymentPeriodGuard` over the shipped `GET /api/v1/periods/by-date` lookup, failing closed on `Closed`, no-period, and unreachable — deliberately NOT repeating the Invoices service's production `AlwaysOpenInvoicePeriodGuard`. The stub exists for tests only. It runs on confirm and on `POST /{id}/reverse`; it is ALSO wired on the operator post's `Confirmed`-AND-linked branch, which is unreachable defense-in-depth because that state is never persisted (§2.5). The back-event link path is exempt by design (§2.9 note).
- **Reverse pre-checks the period at the ENDPOINT, because the Journal's own guard is inert on the reversal path.** `JournalEntryService.ReverseInTransactionAsync` reuses `original.EntryDate` (`BuildReversal`) and calls `StampPosted` directly, and `PostingPeriodWorkflowGuard` short-circuits to success for anything other than `TargetState == Posted` — while the transition performed on the original is `Posted → Reversed`. So without this spec's pre-check, reversing a payment whose period has since closed would silently insert a `Posted` entry into a closed period (§2.7). v1 does NOT re-date the reversing entry; a closed original period is a hard block requiring a reopen (SDD-FIN-004). The generic, all-document-types version of the defect is recorded as `CHG-FIX-004` and is NOT fixed here.
- **Cancel is `Draft`-only; a confirmed-or-later payment is corrected by reversal, never cancelled.** Once `PaymentConfirmedEvent` is in the outbox the posting is irrevocably in flight and the Journal consumer posts regardless of the payment's later state (its only guard is the §2.5 source-document check), so a `Confirmed → Cancelled` transition could strand a posted entry with no supporting document and no correction path (`Cancelled` is terminal; `Reversed` is reachable only from `Posted`). `Confirmed → Cancelled` is therefore absent from `AllowedNextStates` (§2.1) — the same principle SDD-AUDIT-001 applies to posted journal entries. A payment stuck in `Confirmed` is an operational matter resolved by re-driving `POST /{id}/post`, which re-enqueues the confirm event and is repeatable without producing a second journal entry (§2.5, §2.18) — not by cancelling. Cancel and reverse both still require a `Reason`; no UPDATE of a posted payment ever, and the GL correction is a sign-flipped entry produced by SDD-FIN-002's shipped `ReverseAsync`. Cancel/reverse of an allocated payment is rejected (`PAYMENT_HAS_ALLOCATIONS`) so no allocation row can point at a voided payment — deallocate first through SDD-PAY-002.
  - SDD-INV-001 has the identical latent hole on its own `Confirmed → Cancelled` transition. Its shipped, tested lifecycle is NOT changed by this batch; the finding is recorded as `CHG-FIX-005`.
- **"Release-on-reverse" is RESOLVED as a hard block, not an open question.** A payment with `AllocatedAmount > 0` is hard-blocked from BOTH cancel and reverse (§2.6/§2.7, §3.2); allocations are NEVER auto-released, auto-deallocated, or cascade-deleted by this spec. SDD-PAY-002 cites this decision rather than restating it as open.
- **No caching.** Payments are transactional data (SDD-INFRA-004). Redis is registered ONLY for the messaging idempotency multiplexer; no `finance-payments` cache prefix is added.
- **Settlement-account check is existence + active, with a dormant strictness seam.** The Chart of Accounts has no `IsPostable`/`IsHeader` flag, so the reader asserts what exists today and localizes the future strictness in a SINGLE named `IsPostable` predicate on `ISettlementAccountReader` that returns `true` unconditionally in v1 and is pinned by its own `[Unit]` test (§2.8, §6.3) — the same seam pattern SDD-FIN-002 used for its dormant period-lock guard.
- **Bundled adjacent fix, shipping with ASSERTED SIDES rather than as a copy.** The `CREDIT_NOTE` / `DEBIT_NOTE` posting-rule templates left unseeded by SDD-INV-001 §7 since Batch 16 are seeded in the SAME `BulgariaStrategy.GetDefaultPostingRules()` change as the two payment templates (§2.13). Without them note posting keeps failing with `POSTING_RULE_NOT_FOUND`; with them, SDD-INV-001's deferred full-offset reversal follow-up is unblocked. Both templates are specified line-by-line in §2.13 — selector, Debit/Credit side, and `PostingAmountSource` — derived by mirroring the shipped `SALE_INVOICE` (a credit note reverses its sides, a debit note repeats them) and pinned by side-asserting tests (§6.4). Balance alone is NOT an acceptable assertion: `PostingEngine.CheckBalanced` only compares total base debits to total base credits, so an un-flipped copy of `SALE_INVOICE` would pass while making every credit note INCREASE receivables, revenue, and output VAT.

### Open / deferred (post-Batch-17)
- **GATE — the two note templates' accounts MUST have accountant sign-off before `EnablePostingRuleSeeding` is turned on in any environment that has issued a document.** The templates now SHIP in code (§2.13), so this gate is the only thing standing between them and a live rule store. `PostingRuleSeeder` skips an existing `RuleKey` and NEVER overwrites it, and §5 makes changing a seeded rule's selectors or sides a breaking change requiring a `CHG-ENH-*` plus a re-seeding plan — so a wrong `CREDIT_NOTE`/`DEBIT_NOTE` cannot be corrected by re-running the seeder. `BulgariaStrategy` is explicitly SAMPLE/seed data pending accountant sign-off (its own class-level `<summary>` says so — `src/Country/Finance.Country.BG/BulgariaStrategy.cs:11-15` — and SDD-CTRY-001 §2 carries the same caveat over every template it enumerates). The sides in §2.13 are the mirror of the shipped sale-invoice rule and are the technically correct derivation, but the ACCOUNT CODES (`411`/`701`/`4532`) remain sample data. Sign-off is a prerequisite for enabling the flag, not for merging the templates.
- **KNOWN PLATFORM LIMITATION (pre-existing, NOT fixed here) — the general ledger is currency-naive.** `PostingEngine.BuildLine` pins `ExchangeRate = 1.000000m` and sets `BaseDebitAmount`/`BaseCreditAmount` to the amount handed in, while `PostingEngine.DelegateToJournalAsync` stamps the entry's header `BaseCurrencyCode` from `ICountryStrategy.BaseCurrencyCode`. The GL therefore stores the TRANSACTIONAL amount in the base-amount columns for EVERY document type, invoices included — a EUR line on a BGN-base entry reads `BaseDebitAmount = <the EUR figure>`. This is an SDD-FIN-006 limitation that predates this batch and is owned by the not-yet-authored SDD-FIN-005; it MUST NOT be "fixed" by this spec. §2.5's rule (post the transactional amount in the payment's currency) makes payments CONSISTENT with the shipped invoice consumer so control accounts net correctly — it does not make the GL multi-currency-correct, and nothing in this spec should be read as claiming that it does.
- **Operational consequence of the confirm-clock year pinning.** A prior-year payment MUST be confirmed within its own year. Once the calendar year turns, a still-`Draft` prior-year payment can only be completed by reopening the period and re-dating the document into the current year (`PaymentDate` is editable while `Draft`, §2.6). This was chosen deliberately over passing a date into `GenerateDocumentNumber`, which would require a coordinated SDD-INFRA-003 change this batch does not own. Operators MUST be told to close out the year's drafts before year end; a "stale prior-year drafts" report is a candidate for the deferred payments frontend.
- **Cyrillic / method-specific payment numbering is DEFERRED.** `ПКО`/`РКО` are cash-order-specific and would be wrong for `BankTransfer` and `Card`, so they are deferred pending accountant sign-off; changing the format after the first number is issued is irreversible under НАП, so v1 adopts the already-documented Latin format (`RCT-{yyyy}-{nnnnnn}` / `PAY-{yyyy}-{nnnnnn}` — SDD-INFRA-003 §2.1, restated in this spec's §2.4 and §5). Whether a bank-transfer receipt should ever carry a different prefix from a cash receipt (i.e. prefix by `Method`, not only by `DocumentType`) is the open question and belongs to a future SDD-CTRY-BG-001 plus a coordinated SDD-INFRA-003 change spec — it MUST NOT be decided by editing `SequenceDefinitions.cs` in this batch. Decide once, before the first production number is issued.
- **Per-service infrastructure duplication — promote it, do not keep copying. (Debt GREW in Batch 17, as this spec predicted.)** `ICurrentUserAccessor` had FIVE independent per-service copies before this batch (`Finance.Accounts.API`, `Finance.Invoices.API`, `Finance.Journal.API`, `Finance.Nomenclature.API`, `Finance.Periods.API`, each under `<Service>/Interfaces/ICurrentUserAccessor.cs` with its own `HttpContextCurrentUserAccessor`) and `Finance.Payments.API/Interfaces/ICurrentUserAccessor.cs` is now the **SIXTH**. `BearerTokenForwardingHandler` is likewise a THIRD copy (`Finance.Payments.API/Http/`, alongside `Finance.Journal.API/Http/` and `Finance.Nomenclature.API/Http/`), and `IAccountReadClient`/`IPeriodReadClient` each have a SECOND copy under `Finance.Payments.API/Interfaces/` — as §2.8/§2.9 required, since a project reference to another service's API project is forbidden. NONE of them is a shared contract today. The spec-owned **SDD-INFRA-009 change** to promote `ICurrentUserAccessor` + its `HttpContext` implementation, the two Refit read contracts, and `BearerTokenForwardingHandler` into `Finance.Infrastructure.Web` and retire every duplicated copy in one coordinated move is still UNRAISED and MUST be raised before a seventh service is added. This remains a documented recommendation, not an ad-hoc refactor.
- **`Guid` loader vs a shared base-service overload.** `BaseEntityService.FindOrNotFoundAsync` is `int`-keyed while `Payment.Id` is a GUID, so Batch 17 hand-rolled `LoadAsync` in `PaymentService` (as `InvoiceService` does), as recommended. The spec-owned change to SDD-INFRA-009 proposing a `Guid` overload on the shared base is still UNRAISED — it remains a spec-owned change, not an ad-hoc edit.
- **Journal-side reversal consumer placement.** `PaymentReversedEvent` needs an idempotent Journal-side consumer that LOADS the linked entry, takes its base64 `RowVersion`, and then calls `IJournalEntryService.ReverseAsync` (the shipped signature requires a concurrency token the event does not carry) with an already-`Reversed` no-op (§2.7). RESOLVED as recommended: `src/Interfaces/Journal/Finance.Journal.API/Consumers/PaymentReversedEventConsumer.cs` ships as a DEDICATED consumer (mirroring the dedicated-event precedent), with the read-then-reverse and already-`Reversed` no-op behaviors pinned by `PaymentReversedConsumer_*` tests. Whether it is ever generalized into a single "document reversed" consumer shared with a future invoice-reversal event stays OPEN, and MUST NOT be done until a second publisher actually appears.
- **The legacy `CUSTOMER_PAYMENT` sample rule.** Superseded by `PAYMENT_CUSTOMER_RECEIPT` but left in place because the seeder never overwrites and renaming a live key would orphan admin edits. Retiring it (deactivate, do not delete) is a `CHG-DEBT-*`; decide once and record it so the two rules are not both wired.
- **RBAC seeding is manual — and now recorded in a change spec.** The six `finance.payment:read|create|confirm|post|cancel|reverse` permissions this spec introduces MUST be inserted into auth-service by hand until SDD-INT-AUTH-001's auto-registration lands; until someone does, every Payments endpoint returns 403 for every caller. The obligation for the whole Payments surface — these six plus SDD-PAY-002's `finance.payment:allocate` and SDD-PAY-003's `finance.aging:read` — is recorded in `CHG-ENH-003`, discharging SDD-PAY-003 §2.9's requirement that the seeding be recorded in a change spec and not only in code. The only automated guard available is an integration test asserting 403 without the permission, and that test is NOT yet written (§6.7).
- **Nightly reconciliation of `AllocatedAmount`.** This spec owns the column but never writes it. If SDD-PAY-002's event-fed maintenance ever drifts, a reconciliation job recomputing `AllocatedAmount` from the allocation rows would live with that spec — recorded here so the ownership boundary is unambiguous.
